How to edit your pages, the easy way

You do not need to be a developer. Almost everything you will ever want to change is plain text that you can find with the search box of your editor. This guide shows you where each thing is and exactly what to type.

0Start here

The three rules

  1. Make a copy first. Before you change a file, copy it and keep the original somewhere safe. If you ever get stuck, you go back to the copy.
  2. Use a real text editor on a computer. The free Visual Studio Code is a good choice. Notepad (Windows) also works. On a Mac do not use TextEdit unless you choose Format → Make Plain Text. Word, Pages and Google Docs must not be used: they damage the file.
  3. Save, then look. After each change, save the file and open it in your browser (double-click it, or refresh the page). If it looks right, carry on. If it looks wrong, press undo (Ctrl+Z) and try again.

1Change any sentence on the website

  1. Open index.html in your editor.
  2. Press Ctrl+F and type a few words from the middle of the sentence, as you see it on the page.
  3. Change the words. Save. Refresh the page in your browser.

Some sentences have small markers around a word, for example <em>change hands.</em>. The <em> makes those words gold and italic. Keep the markers (everything between < and >) and change only the words.

CarefulDo not type the sign < inside a sentence, and do not delete a < or > that belongs to a marker. The sign & is fine.

2Add your email and links

This is the one job you must do before the website goes live. Search for EDIT: SETTINGS in index.html. You will find this:

const CONFIG = {
    email: '',
    formEndpoint: '',
    bookingUrl: '',
    social: { linkedin: '', youtube: '', instagram: '', x: '', whatsapp: '' }
  };

Type between the two small quote marks ' ', like this:

const CONFIG = {
    email: 'hello@yourcompany.com',
    formEndpoint: '',
    bookingUrl: 'https://cal.com/your-name',
    social: { linkedin: 'https://www.linkedin.com/company/your-page', youtube: '', instagram: '', x: '', whatsapp: '' }
  };
LineWhat it does
emailWhen a visitor sends the contact form, their email app opens with the message ready, addressed to you.
formEndpointOptional and better: the web address of a form service (for example Formspree) that delivers the message straight to your inbox. If you fill this in, it is used instead of email.
bookingUrlOptional: a “Book a call” button appears when you add your calendar link.
socialEach network you fill in becomes a real link. The empty ones show a quiet “soon” label, so nothing looks broken.
Test itOpen the page, fill in the contact form and send it to yourself. Until email or formEndpoint is set, the form tells the visitor honestly that it is not connected yet. It never pretends to send.
CarefulKeep the small quote marks and the comma at the end of each line. Always write a full web address, starting with https://.

4Change a photo or add a new person’s photo

The photos live inside the page file, so there is a small helper that does the hard part for you. It runs in your browser and never uploads anything.

  1. Double-click tools/photo-swapper.html.
  2. Step 1: choose your page (index.html or the blueprint).
  3. Step 2: tap the person (or picture) you want to change. To add a new person, type a short name in letters, for example sara, and press Add this person.
  4. Step 3: choose the new picture, move the sliders until the face sits inside the bright circle, then press Use this picture.
  5. Step 4: press Download the updated page. Replace your old file with the downloaded one (keep the same name). Open it to check.
New person?After adding the photo, write the same short name next to photo: in that person’s block (Team members).
Good photosA clear photo of the face, taller or square, with the person looking at the camera. The other photos are black and white, so keep “Black and white” ticked to match.

5Change the readiness-check questions

Search for EDIT: CHECK QUESTIONS. There are four questions and four answers each, ordered from “most dependent on the owner” (first) to “fully ready” (last). Change only the words between the double quote marks:

{ id: "owner", icon: "i-key", name: "Owner dependency", axis: "Owner independence",
  q: "If you stepped away for three months, what would happen to the business?",
  opts: [
    "It would stall: most decisions and key relationships run through me.",
    "It would struggle: the team could only cope with routine tasks.",
    "It would slow down, but others could handle most decisions.",
    "It would keep running: others decide and own the key relationships."
  ] },
CarefulKeep every text inside its double quote marks, keep the comma after every answer except the last, and never type a straight " inside a text (use the curly “ ”). The result messages and tips that appear after the check are just above, under EDIT: MESSAGES.

6Add or change a question in the FAQ

  1. Search for SECTION: FAQ.
  2. You will see one block per question, starting with <details and ending with </details>. To change one, change the words of the question and of the answer.
  3. To add one, copy a whole block and paste it right after another block. Then change the words. This is the empty block to copy:
<details class="faq-item reveal"><summary><span>Your question here?</span><i class="faq-ic" aria-hidden="true"></i></summary><div class="faq-a"><p>Your answer here.</p></div></details>

The same idea works for the choices of the contact form (“I am…”, “Handover I have in mind”, “Timing”): search for <option and change only the words between > and <. Leave value="…" as it is.

7Colours, page title and the preview when you share the link

Colours

Search for --gold:. The colours are in one short list at the top. Change the code after the colon (for example #C9A24B). Any colour picker website gives you these codes.

The name in the browser tab and in link previews

At the very top of the file, search for <title>. Change the words between <title> and </title>. Directly below you will find the short description used by search engines and chat apps: <meta name="description" content="…">. Change only the words inside the quote marks after content=. The same text appears twice more in the lines that start with <meta property="og:; change those too so previews match.

9Publish and share

  • Send the file. Attach index.html to an email or a chat. The person double-clicks it and the website opens. It needs no internet connection.
  • Put it online. Any website host works, because the website is just one file. Upload index.html and name it exactly index.html so the address needs no file name. Most hosts have an “upload your site” button; many are free for a page like this.
  • GitHub Pages (if your repository allows it): Repository → Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch, choose the branch and the / (root) folder, then save. The website appears at https://<your-user>.github.io/<repository>/power-of-us/.
Keep the plan privateThe social media blueprint and the tools are for your team. Upload only index.html to a public site, and share the rest directly with your team (zip the folder).

10If something looks wrong

  1. Undo. Press Ctrl+Z in your editor until the page is right again, or put back your copy of the file.
  2. Look for the usual slips. A missing quote mark or comma in the settings or the check questions, a deleted < or > in a sentence, a straight " typed inside a text, or a - line in the team block without a space after it.
  3. Ask the browser. Press F12, open Console. A red line tells you what went wrong, usually with the line number.
Safety netIf the script on the website ever fails, the page falls back to a plain, readable version instead of going blank. So a small slip never breaks everything.

11Before you share it with clients

  1. Send yourself a test message through the contact form and check that it arrives.
  2. Click every social link and the booking button.
  3. Each person has approved their photo, name, role and words.
  4. Read the statements that were written for you to approve:
    • the line under the first button: “Early-stage project, now opening first pilot conversations with owner-led SMEs.”
    • the privacy line in the contact section: “We use your details only to reply to you.”
    • the sentences saying this is an independent project and not an official HEC Paris service
    • Alhanouf’s quote, key words, “how to work with me” and strengths. Her slide in the deck repeated another member’s text, so these were written from her role and the words on her own slide. Please replace them with her own words.
  5. Look at the website on a phone and on a computer, and check the spelling.
