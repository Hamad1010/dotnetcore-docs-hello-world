05Pathways

Where is your business heading next?

Whatever the destination, the first step is the same: a business that can run without you. Choose the handover you have in mind.

Family succession

Hand it to the next generation.

Your children, or the family’s next leaders, should inherit a business, not a burden. Readiness is how you give them the first.

  • Make the business runnable by someone who is not you
  • Turn what lives in your head into documented routines and clear decision rights
  • Clarify roles and governance early, while there is still time to adjust
  • Modernize how the company works so the next generation inherits momentum

    10Questions

    Straight answers, no jargon.

    Are you a consulting firm, a broker or a law firm?

    No. We do not provide legal, tax, valuation, M&A or brokerage services; those stay with qualified specialists. We focus on the unowned layer in between: making the business ready to run, and to be handed over, without depending on its owner.

    Will you replace my current advisors?

    No. The model works alongside trusted advisors, not instead of them. A Future-Ready Transition Lead coordinates one first action with the people you already trust.

    I am not handing over for several years. Is it too early?

    It is rarely too early. Owners aged 60 and over tend to under-invest, and the company loses transferable value while they wait; 70% have not started preparing even when the transfer is more than a year away. Readiness takes time, so start early.

    Who is behind The Power of Us?

    Team 4 of HEC Paris EMSIE, Cohort 17: six professionals from France, Peru, Morocco and Saudi Arabia. You can meet everyone in the team section above.

    What stage is the project at?

    Early stage. We have completed a full design-thinking journey and first evidence tests: owner demand and alumni supply each cleared a bar we fixed in advance, while the trust model is designed but not yet run. Next comes an end-to-end pilot.

    What does it cost?

    Pricing is part of what we are validating: willingness to pay is one of our next tests. Start a conversation and we will be transparent about what we know today.

    Is my information confidential?

    The readiness check runs entirely in your browser. If you contact us, we use your details only to reply to you.
