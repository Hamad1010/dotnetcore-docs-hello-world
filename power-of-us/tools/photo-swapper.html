Photo swapper

Change a photo or picture inside your page without touching any code. Everything happens on your computer; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1 Choose your page

Pick index.html (the website) or social-media-blueprint.html. Keep a copy of the original file first, just in case.

2 What do you want to change?

Tap the picture you want to replace.

Or add a new person

After that, write the same name next to photo: in that person’s block in the TEAM PROFILES (see the guide).

3 Choose the new picture

The website shows people in a circle. Keep the face inside the bright circle.

4 Save the updated page

Changes made so far:

    Your browser saves the file in its Downloads folder. Replace the old file with it (keep the same name), then open it in your browser to check. You can change more pictures first; the download always contains all your changes.
