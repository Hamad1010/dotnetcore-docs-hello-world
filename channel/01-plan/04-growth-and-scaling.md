# 04 · Growth and scaling plan

Plan for surprises before they happen. This is the honest math, the phases, the numbers we watch and how
the system scales without you working more hours.

## 1. The monetization math (read this first)

**Today (until 31 Jan 2027):** to join the YouTube Partner Program (ads revenue) you need **1,000 subscribers +
4,000 public watch hours in 12 months**, or 1,000 subscribers + 10M Shorts views in 90 days.

**From 1 Feb 2027 (announced Aug 2026):** new applicants need **1,000 subscribers + 8,000 watch hours in 12
months**, or **20M Shorts views in 90 days**. Channels already in the program keep their status.

What that means in views (our videos ≈ 6.5 min, assume viewers watch ~45% ≈ 2.9 min):

| Target | Watch minutes | ≈ Long-video views needed |
|---|---|---|
| 4,000 hours (old rule) | 240,000 | ~83,000 views |
| 8,000 hours (new rule) | 480,000 | ~165,000 views |

**Honest outlook:** reaching the old threshold before 1 Feb 2027 (≈16 weeks from now) would need an average of
~3,500 views on each of ~24 videos. That's possible only with an early breakout, so treat it as a **bonus, not
the plan**. The plan assumes the **8,000-hour rule** and aims to qualify in **9–15 months**. Many channels never
qualify. Our edge is consistency, a clear format and a fully planned library of 330 lessons.

**Levers that add watch hours fastest (all allowed, all original):**
1. **Season compilations** of our own episodes ("كل أسرار الكاريزما في 40 دقيقة"), one per finished season.
2. **Longer hero episodes** (10–12 min) on the topics that perform best.
3. **Live Q&A** in Saudi dialect once a month. Public live replays count toward watch hours.
4. **Playlists + end screens + next-lesson teasers** so one view becomes three.

Shorts views **don't** count toward watch hours, but Shorts bring the subscribers who watch long videos.

## 3. The numbers we watch (weekly review)

| Metric | Healthy for a new channel | If below, do this |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions CTR | 4–8% | Test new thumbnails/titles; make the promise sharper |
| Retention at 0:30 | ≥ 65% | Rewrite the hook; show the number earlier |
| Average % viewed (AVP) | ≥ 40% | Cut slow parts; move best stories earlier; shorten stories |
| Returning viewers | rising month over month | More series links, end screens, community posts |
| Subscribers per 1,000 views | ≥ 5 | Stronger value promise + "next lesson" teaser |
| Shorts swipe-away | falling | Hook in the first second; text on the first frame |
| Comments per 1,000 views | ≥ 3 | Sharper exercise question; reply faster |

**Decision rules** (no guessing):
- A topic beats the channel average 2× → make 3 more lessons from that season next.
- 3 videos in a row below 2% CTR → packaging problem, not content: change the thumbnail style.
- Retention cliff at the same spot in 3 videos → template problem: fix it in the studio once, and every future video improves.

## 4. Scaling production (time stays ~3 h/week for you)

1. **Templates first:** one `episode.json` = one video. The format stays fixed and the stories change.
2. **Claude skills:** `/new-episode` drafts, researches, fact-checks and writes the publish kit; `/weekly-review` turns numbers into decisions.
3. **Batching:** write 4 scripts in one session, voice 4, render 4.
4. **Voice clone:** once your voice is cloned, you approve scripts instead of recording them.
5. **Rendering:** your laptop renders a 6-min video in ~10–20 min. At 3+ videos/week consider Remotion Lambda (cloud rendering, pennies per video).
6. **People (phase 3):** a part-time researcher (stories + sources) and a Shorts editor. The checklists in `05-templates/` are their job description.

## 5. Scaling distribution

- Every long video becomes 3 YouTube Shorts, then the same clips go to TikTok, Snapchat Spotlight and Instagram Reels (same week, no watermark).
- X: a thread per episode (the 8 stories with numbers + link).
- LinkedIn: the work/leadership lessons (season 9) as short posts for managers and HR.
- WhatsApp channel: one lesson card per week. It's an audience we own if a platform changes its rules.

## 6. Scaling revenue (don't depend on ads alone)

Arabic ad rates are modest: estimates for Saudi audiences range roughly **$1.5–6 per 1,000 views** (sources
disagree). 100k views/month ≈ $150–600. So we stack income:

| Stage | Income | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| After YPP | Ads, Super Thanks, memberships | Memberships: "early access + monthly live Q&A" |
| ~10k subs | Sponsorships (needs **Mawthooq** license) | Education, books, apps, banks' financial wellness |
| ~10k subs | Digital products | Printable workbook per season (exercises from the videos) |
| ~25k subs | Workshops for companies/schools | EI and communication for teams. Biggest income per hour |
| Later | English-dubbed channel or audio track | Same videos, global audience, higher ad rates |
| With permission | Partnership with the source course owner | Co-branded series or promoting his full courses |

## 7. Risks and how we're protected

| Risk | Protection |
|---|---|
| YouTube AI/"inauthentic content" enforcement | Original research, human review, own voice, distinctive format, sources in description |
| Copyright claims | Own music; facts not footage; no copied text; permission from the course owner |
| Monetization rules tighten again (they did in 2026) | Diversified income + owned WhatsApp audience |
| A video spreads with a factual error | `sources.md`, correction pinned in comments, fix and re-upload if serious |
| Burnout | 2-week buffer, batching, the voice clone |
| Account hacked | 2-step verification, brand account, no attachments from "sponsors" |
| Dependence on one platform | Repurpose to 4 platforms + WhatsApp channel |
