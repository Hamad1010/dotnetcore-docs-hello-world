# 08 · Coaching roadmap: your first 90 days, step by step

How we work: each step has **what you learn**, **your action** (kept short) and **what Claude does**. Don't
skip ahead. Each step builds the habit the next one needs.

---

## Week 0: Foundations (this week)

**Step 1 · Understand the machine (15 min read)**
- *Learn:* how a video is made here. `episode.json` (words + story data) → voice → Remotion (React code that draws every frame) → MP4. You never edit video timelines; you edit *text*.
- *Action:* open `03-episodes/EP001/script.md` and `episode.json` side by side. Notice how each story has `stats`, `beats` and a `takeaway`.
- *Claude:* answers any "why is it built like this?" question.

**Step 2 · Judge the test video (20 min)**
- *Learn:* how to review like a producer. Hook, pace, clarity, story strength, the ending.
- *Action:* watch `EP001.mp4` once as a viewer and once with a pen. Write 5 notes: keep / cut / change.
- *Claude:* applies your notes to the template so *every* future episode improves.

**Step 3 · Three decisions (30 min)**
- Channel name + handle (`01-channel-blueprint.md` §2)
- Voice: your own voice / your clone / AI voice (§5)
- Upload days and time (default Mon + Thu 8–9 pm KSA)

**Step 4 · Setup (45 min, once)** → `09-setup-guide.md`. Includes creating a private repo for this project.

**Step 5 · Permission** → send the request to the course owner (`05-templates/permission-request.md`).

## Week 1: Make episodes 2–4 (build the buffer)

- *Learn:* the `/new-episode` workflow, and how to fact-check a story (read `05-templates/story-research.md`).
- *Action:* say "run `/new-episode`" 3 times (EP002–EP004). Review each script in 10 min: is every sentence simple Saudi dialect? Is each story true and surprising?
- *Claude:* researches 5–10 stories per lesson with sources, writes scripts, renders previews, writes `publish.md`.
- *Voice:* if you chose your own voice, record EP001–EP004 (one file per scene; ~15 min per episode).

## Week 2: Channel ready

- *Learn:* channel setup that works for discovery: banner, About section, upload defaults, playlists, trailer.
- *Action:* create the Brand Account with 2-step verification; upload art; create the "الموسم 1" playlist; set upload defaults.
- *Claude:* renders the trailer, banner text and About copy; writes the first 12 Shorts scripts.

## Weeks 3–6: Launch and learn (Mon 26 Oct = launch day)

- *Learn:* reading YouTube Studio. CTR, the retention graph, traffic sources, returning viewers.
- *Action (each publish day, 25 min):* upload → Arabic subtitles (`subtitles.ar.srt`) → chapters → 3 thumbnails in Test & Compare → pin the question comment → reply to comments in the first hour → post the Short.
- *Friday weekly review (20 min):* fill `05-templates/weekly-review.md` or paste the Studio numbers to Claude ("run `/weekly-review`").
- *Claude:* turns your numbers into 1–3 concrete changes for next week, no more.

## Weeks 7–12: Double down

- *Learn:* outlier thinking. Which lesson types beat your average, and why.
- *Action:* approve the Season 2 theme from the data; record your voice sample for cloning (if chosen).
- *Claude:* builds a vertical (9:16) Shorts template in the studio so Shorts render automatically from each episode; starts season compilations.
- *Milestone check (day 90):* 24+ long videos, 60+ Shorts, a buffer of 4 episodes, weekly reviews done. Count these, not only subscribers.

---

## Skills you'll have after 90 days

1. Writing a hook and a title that earn the click.
2. Spotting a weak story in a script before it costs you retention.
3. Reading a retention graph and fixing the exact second people leave.
4. Running a channel in ~3 hours a week with a repeatable system.
5. Knowing the rules (monetization, AI, copyright, Mawthooq) well enough to never get surprised.

## When you're stuck

Tell Claude what you see, not what you think the fix is: "views are low on EP005", "people leave at 0:40".
Then ask "what are the 3 most likely causes and the cheapest test for each?"
