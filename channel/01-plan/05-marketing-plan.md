# 05 · Marketing plan (logical, honest, low-budget)

## Principles

1. **The video is the marketing.** No promotion saves a weak video, and a strong video gets pushed by YouTube itself. 80% of effort goes into packaging and content, 20% into distribution.
2. **Go where the audience already is.** In Saudi Arabia that's YouTube, Snapchat, TikTok, X and WhatsApp. Saudi Arabia has one of the highest YouTube usage rates in the world (around 96% of internet users, per Statista figures cited in 2026 roundups).
3. **Build an audience we own** (WhatsApp channel and later email), so a platform change can't wipe us out.
4. **Don't buy fake growth.** No bought subs/views, no sub4sub groups. They poison the metrics YouTube uses to recommend you.
5. **Paid ads only on proven videos**, later, small and measured.

## Collaborations (the fastest honest growth)

1. **Saudi trainers, coaches and psychologists** on Snapchat/X: "Duet" style Shorts, guest stories, live Q&A.
2. **University student clubs** (communication/leadership clubs): free workshop + share the series with members.
3. **HR departments and schools:** the family (season 14) and work (seasons 12–13) lessons as ready-made training clips.
4. **Arabic education creators** in adjacent niches (psychology, productivity, history): story swaps. Their
   historic story becomes our lesson story, credited both ways.

## Search (free, compounding traffic)

- Title: keyword in the first half ("الكاريزما", "الذكاء العاطفي", "فن الرد"…).
- Description: first 2 lines = the promise + keyword; then chapters (auto-generated `chapters.txt`), sources, and links to the season playlist.
- Upload `subtitles.ar.srt` (auto-generated). Captions help search, accessibility and viewers on mute.
- 5–8 relevant tags only (tags matter little; don't stuff them).

## Paid promotion (optional, later)

- Only after a video beats the channel average in CTR **and** retention for 7 days.
- Start with **SAR 100–200** via YouTube Studio's "Promote" or a Google Ads video campaign targeting KSA, 18–34, interests: self-development, education.
- Success = cost per *engaged* view and subscribers per riyal, not views. Stop if one subscriber costs more than ~SAR 2–3.
- Never promote to "get monetized faster": paid views count, but low-quality views hurt retention signals.
