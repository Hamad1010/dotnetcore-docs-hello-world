# 07 · Things you didn't ask for (but will make the channel stronger)

Ranked by impact. ✅ = already built into this system.

## Must-do before launch

1. **Ask the course owner for permission (and a collaboration).** It's the biggest legal *and* growth lever you have. Template message: `05-templates/permission-request.md`.
2. **Move the project to a private repository.** The current GitHub repo is a public fork, so anyone can read your plans and scripts.
3. **Brand account + 2-step verification + backup owner.** Hacked channels are common, usually via fake sponsor emails.
4. **Upload defaults** (YouTube Studio → Settings → Upload defaults): category *Education*, language *Arabic*, a description template with your links/WhatsApp channel, "not made for kids", comments "hold potentially inappropriate for review". This saves time on every upload.
5. **Channel trailer (45 s)** for non-subscribers: the promise, 3 fast story clips, "a new lesson every Monday and Thursday". Claude can render it from EP001's scenes.
6. **A human signature.** Faceless works, but people trust a person. Say who you are in the trailer (first name is enough) and end every video with the same sign-off line, e.g. «وتذكّر: الذكاء الحقيقي… إنك تفهم الناس».

## Production leverage

7. ✅ **One template, many videos.** `episode.json` → video, thumbnail, subtitles, chapters. Fix the template once and every future video improves.
8. ✅ **Story bank** (`02-lessons/story-bank.csv`): every verified story with its sources and the lessons it can teach. One story can serve several lessons from different angles, so research gets cheaper every month. Don't reuse a story within 20 episodes.
9. ✅ **Numbers spoken as words, shown as digits** (`{1987|ألف وتسعمية…}`): natural Saudi speech, clean captions.
10. **Voice clone of yourself** after episode 3: your authenticity at AI speed.
11. **Batch days:** 4 scripts → 4 voices → 4 renders in one sitting. Context-switching is the real time thief.
12. **Swipe file:** keep the best-performing titles/thumbnails (yours and others') in `05-templates/swipe-file.md`, and learn from outliers.

## Growth multipliers

13. **Interactive tests** (season-1 finale: "اختبر ذكاءك الاجتماعي في 16 سؤال"). Quizzes get massive comments and shares; later, a free web version of the test that links back to the videos.
14. **Season compilations** (one long video per finished season). Big watch-hour boost for monetization.
15. **"Reply to comment" Shorts:** answer a viewer's real situation using the lesson. It shows you listen and creates endless ideas.
16. **Community polls:** "Which lesson next?" Viewers who vote come back to see the result.
17. **Monthly live Q&A** (audio + slides is enough): watch hours + loyalty.
18. **Podcast feed for free:** the voice tracks + music as an audio podcast (Spotify/Apple). Long drives in Saudi = big podcast listening.
19. **English edition later:** same visuals, English voice or YouTube's multi-language audio. Global audience, 3–5× higher ad rates.
20. **WhatsApp channel + printable lesson cards** (one PDF per season). This is an audience you own, and families forward it.

## Trust and safety

21. ✅ **Sources in every description** + a pinned correction if we ever get something wrong.
22. **Comment moderation:** blocked-words list (insults, spam links), "hold for review" on. Reply to criticism calmly. It's on-brand (we teach this!).
23. **Sensitive lessons** (grief, fear, self-worth): a gentle line pointing to specialists, and no clinical claims.
24. **No politics, no sectarian topics, no mocking any culture**, even when a story is tempting.

## Money readiness

25. **Media kit** (1 page) once you pass ~5k subs: audience, views, topics, contact. Claude can generate it from YouTube Studio numbers.
26. **Mawthooq license** before the first paid sponsorship (`06-monetization-policy-legal.md` §5).
27. **AdSense tax form (W-8BEN)** on day one of monetization to avoid 24% backup withholding.
28. **B2B workshops:** companies and schools pay for emotional-intelligence and communication training. Your channel *is* the portfolio. This often earns more than ads.

## Personal sustainability

29. **Process goals over vanity goals:** "ship 2 videos/week for 12 weeks" is in your control; views aren't.
30. **A 2-week buffer** of finished episodes so travel, illness or Ramadan never break the schedule.
