# 13 · The words people use now, and what's trending (July–October 2026)

Written 21 Oct 2026 for the launch kit of EP001 (titles, descriptions, thumbnails). Reuse the method for every episode.

## 1. What this is, and what it is not

The owner asked that titles, descriptions and thumbnails use the words viewers use for what they're struggling with now, based on the
top YouTube videos of the last three months and on Google's and YouTube's own tools. Here is what could and couldn't be done.

**Could not (blocked from the build session):** YouTube search suggestions, Google Trends, YouTube Studio's Research tab, YouTube's help
pages. The session's network policy denied `suggestqueries.google.com`, `trends.google.com`, `www.youtube.com`, `support.google.com` and
`www.google.com`. So **there are no search-volume numbers here**, and nobody should read any into this file. Adding those hosts to the
environment's allowed domains (cloud environment menu → Edit → Network access) lets the research be redone with live data.

**Did:** web searches (Arabic and English) for Saudi surveys and statistics, Google's Year in Search, 2026 title and thumbnail guides, YouTube
platform news, and a sample of what Arabic YouTube titles currently look like for the words in question. Search results are not YouTube's ranking
and not a count of views, so they show **how the niche talks**, not **how big each word is**.

What this says about EP001: the strongest, best-supported pressure is **money and time** (living costs, saving, long hours) and **a pull toward

## 3. How the Arabic YouTube niche looks right now

A sample of what web search surfaced for each query on 21 Oct 2026 (titles only; the sample is small and not ranked by views).

- **«مشروع من الجوال بدون رأس مال السعودية 2026»** → 10 titles: 8 contain «2026»; 7 contain «رأس مال» (3 of them «بدون رأس مال»); most are
  lists («أفضل 10/15 فكرة»), a few add «(الكل يتجاهله!)» or an amount («يبدأ من 50$»).
- **«كيف تبدأ مشروع وأنت موظف دخل إضافي الذكاء الاصطناعي»** → 10 titles, all about earning: four name an amount («+7500 ريال شهريًا»,
  «+3000 دولار شهريًا», «$1000», «800 دولار»), one says «دخل شهري مضمون», one «قد تحقق لك ثروة». That is the kind of promise YouTube's
  scams policy warns against and our own rules forbid, and it's why EP001's packaging promises none.
- **«الراتب ما يكفي كيف أزيد دخلي»** → the words are «زيادة دخلك», «دخل إضافي», «مصادر دخل», «بدون ما تترك وظيفتك», «من البيت», «ابدأ من اليوم».
- **«واتساب للأعمال الردود السريعة»** → plain tutorials (how to set up auto-replies, a greeting message, a catalog), no story.
- **«الرافعة المالية»** → every result is about trading and forex. **«الرافعة» on its own pulls the wrong audience.** It is the reason no title says it
  alone, and every description has a line saying it is not financial leverage.
- **The WhatsApp and Kodak stories** exist in Arabic as «قصة صعود مؤسس واتساب… 19 مليار» (about Jan Koum) and many «قصة إفلاس كوداك». I found
  no Arabic video built on Brian Acton's rejection by Facebook, and none setting Kodak's 140,000 against Instagram's 13. (A search tool isn't an
  index of YouTube, so «found none» is not «none exists».)

**So what:** the niche is crowded with idea lists and money promises. A true story with numbers, an honest «no promise» tone and a clear idea is
what is missing; and the shared phrases («بدون رأس مال», «دخل», «من الجوال», «الذكاء الاصطناعي») are worth carrying, because that is how people ask.

## 4. What is working for titles and thumbnails (2026 guides)

These come from creator-tool vendors and trade press, not controlled studies; they agree with each other and with YouTube's own direction.

- **Titles:** about 60 characters are visible on a phone, so the hook and the main words go first; one honest promise; a specific number where it adds
  information; no shouting. The «longer vs shorter» debate is unresolved, so test.
- **Thumbnails:** one focal point, few words, large type, strong contrast, a consistent look across the channel; text must not cover the subject;
  remember that people see it small (a phone feed, a sidebar) or far (a TV).
- **Retention counts as much as clicks:** a thumbnail that gets clicks and then loses viewers earns less reach than a quieter one that keeps them.
  YouTube's **Test & compare** (up to three thumbnails; titles too, where available) picks by watch time, not clicks alone.
- **Misleading packaging is enforced:** YouTube said in Dec 2024 it would raise enforcement against titles and thumbnails that promise what the video
  doesn't deliver.

## 5. Platform news from the last three months

- **Mid-July 2026** (TechCrunch reported it on 20 Jul): YouTube clarified its monetization rules on «AI slop» (generic or template content, unsatisfying
  content, synthetic hosts giving advice on health, money or law). Original, own-voice content like ours is not the target.
- **10 Aug 2026:** the Partner Program change taking effect on 1 Feb 2027 (new applicants will need 8,000 watch hours or 20 million Shorts views;
  subscribers stay at 1,000). The launch guide's section 5 builds on it.
- **July 2026:** an updated Studio began rolling out; labels and availability of the Research tab's «content gaps» vary by channel, device and country.

## 7. Ten minutes in YouTube Studio to turn this into numbers

1. Studio → Analytics → **Research**. Type each phrase: «دخل إضافي», «مشروع بدون رأس مال», «واتساب للأعمال», «الذكاء الاصطناعي للمشاريع», «ساعات العمل».
   Note the volume (low, medium, high) and any «content gap» (few or poor results). Availability varies after the July 2026 Studio update.
2. **Google Trends** → Explore → Saudi Arabia → past 90 days: compare the five phrases and read «Related queries → Rising».
3. YouTube's **search box**: type each phrase and write down the suggestions (they're what people actually type).
4. If a close phrase is clearly bigger than one in a title («دخل إضافي» vs «زيادة دخلك»), swap it, but only if the video really covers it.
5. Do this again for each episode before writing its title.
