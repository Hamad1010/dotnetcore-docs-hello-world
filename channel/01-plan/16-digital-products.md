# 16 · Digital products: one PDF per episode, from season 2

Written 10 Nov 2026 from the owner's notes. Facts marked **(verify)** come from law-firm or vendor summaries, not the official
text: check them on the official site, or with an accountant or lawyer, before launch. Nothing here is legal or tax advice.

## 1. The product

**«دليل الملف»**, one PDF per season-2 episode, for people who watched and want to practise. It goes where the video
can't: the episode has 4–8 stories; the PDF has the exercises.

| Section | What's in it | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The map | the episode's principle on one page, and how to use the guide in 14 days | 1–2 |
| 2. What the video didn't say | the deeper rules behind each story: when they work, when they fail | 6–8 |
| 3. Scenarios | 12–20 practice scenarios across the five audiences, each with «وش لو…؟» branches: the usual move → what it costs → the right move → why | 15–20 |
| 4. Ready words | phrases and messages to adapt (an opener, a follow-up, an ask), with what to change for each situation | 3–4 |
| 5. 14-day plan | one small action a day, with a box to tick, and a check-in on day 7 and day 14 | 2 |
| 6. Self-check | before and after, to see the change | 1 |
| 7. The evidence | every study, survey and statistic used, with authors, year, sample and link | 3–5 |

About 35–45 pages, A4, Arabic right to left, in the channel's watercolor look; a lighter phone edition later if buyers ask.

Same rules as the videos: every fact sourced, every imagined case labelled «مثال توضيحي», no income promises, and nothing
taken from private course material (CLAUDE.md rule 2). Names in Arabic and English where they are foreign.

## 2. The price: 99 SAR is fair, launched well

- **99 SAR** is a normal price for a serious practical guide in Saudi Arabia, and the scenarios carry the value.
- **Launch:** 79 SAR for the first 7 days or the first 100 buyers, then 99. It rewards the people who act on the video and
  gives the list a reason to open the launch email.
- **Bundle:** the five season-2 guides for 349 SAR (about 30% off), once three exist.
- **Show the price with VAT included** once you are VAT-registered (below), so the price never changes at checkout.
- **A promise you can keep:** "if the guide doesn't help you in 14 days, write to us" builds trust. Decide the refund policy
  before launch and show it on the product page.

## 3. Legal and tax in Saudi Arabia

| Item | What to do |
|---|---|
| Who sells | A **freelance document** (وثيقة العمل الحر, free, issued online by the Ministry of Human Resources) or a commercial registration. Check that its activity list covers selling digital content or training material **(verify)**. |
| E-commerce law | The store page must show who you are, how to contact you, the full price and the refund policy. Salla and Zid build this in. |
| VAT (15%) | Registration is mandatory past **SAR 375,000** of sales a year and allowed from **SAR 187,500** **(verify with ZATCA)**. Below that, no VAT. |
| Personal data (PDPL) | Marketing needs **opt-in consent**, separate from the purchase; opt-out must be free and as easy as opting in; stop within 24 hours of an opt-out; keep proof of consent; publish a privacy policy **(verify: the implementing regulations are being amended)**. |
| WhatsApp and SMS | Marketing only to people who opted in; also follow the CST anti-spam rules and WhatsApp's own policy. |
| Advertising licence | Promoting your own product on your own channel is different from paid ads for others; confirm whether the «موثوق» licence applies to you **(verify)**. |
| YouTube | Selling your own product is allowed; put the link in the description and the pinned comment. Paid promotion: No, since it's your own product. Links need the channel's advanced features (phone-verified). |

## 4. The store

**Recommended: Salla (سلة) or Zid (زد).** Both are Saudi, sell digital products, take mada, Apple Pay and STC Pay (plus
Tabby or Tamara), are in Arabic, and collect the buyer's name, email and phone at checkout.

- Zid lists card fees of 1% + 1 SAR up to 3.3% + 1 SAR depending on the method (VAT not included) **(verify on zid.sa)**.
- Salla has help pages on adding a digital product and protecting it **(verify which plan includes downloads)**.
- **Protect the file:** a download link that expires, a limit on downloads, and the buyer's name or email printed small in
  the page footer, which discourages sharing. If the store can't stamp it, I can produce stamped copies per order.
- International buyers later: Payhip (PDF stamping built in), paid through PayPal **(verify payouts to Saudi banks)**.

## 5. Emails and mobile numbers, with permission

**At checkout** (needed to deliver the guide): name, email, phone. **Then a separate, unticked box:**
«أبغى توصلني رسائل عن الحلقات والأدلة الجديدة على الإيميل والواتساب. أقدر ألغي الاشتراك متى ما بغيت.»
Only people who tick it go on the marketing lists. Keep the date, time, page and wording of each consent.

**Before anyone buys:** a free two-page **«ورقة الملف»** (the episode's checklist) in exchange for an email, linked in the
video description. It builds the list from season 2's first day.

**Email:** MailerLite (free up to 1,000 subscribers) or Brevo (free up to 300 emails a day, and it also sends WhatsApp)
**(verify current free limits)**. Connect the store to it with Zapier or Make, sending **only** the people who ticked the box.

**WhatsApp, in three steps:**
1. **A WhatsApp Channel** for new videos and guides: free, one-way, and followers never share their number with you. Start here.
2. **The WhatsApp Business app** for one-to-one replies to buyers.
3. **The WhatsApp Business Platform** through a Saudi provider (Unifonic or Taqnyat, for example) when the list is large:
   approved message templates, opted-in numbers only, paid per message. Saudi marketing messages cost about $0.05–0.06
   each in 2026 **(verify on Meta's rate card)**.

Numbers to watch each week: views → description clicks → visits → sales (conversion), list growth, email opens, refunds.
