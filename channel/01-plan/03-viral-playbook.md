# 03 · Viral playbook (what makes people click, stay, comment and share)

"Viral" isn't luck. It's **packaging × retention × shareability**, repeated every week. Here is the recipe
we use, with 2026 platform features.

## 1. Titles (≤60 characters, keyword early, a question in the viewer's head)

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| Why-question about everyday life | ليش نحب بعض الناس من أول لقاء؟ |
| Number + secret | سر 0.1 ثانية اللي يحدد انطباع الناس عنك |
| "Stop doing X" | لا ترد على الاستفزاز بهالطريقة |
| Smart reply / situation | إذا أحد قال لك "ليش ما تزوجت؟"… رد كذا |
| Test yourself | اختبر ذكاءك الاجتماعي في 16 سؤال |
| Story promise | 8 قصص حقيقية تثبت إن الرحمة أقوى من القوة |

Rules: put the searchable words (الكاريزما، الذكاء العاطفي، فن التعامل، الثقة بالنفس) in the first half. Avoid
ALL-CAPS hype, and never promise what the video doesn't deliver. Give YouTube **3 title options**
(Test & Compare).

## 2. Thumbnails

- **1 idea, ≤5 words, 1 visual** (big number or globe), yellow badge, lesson number top corner.
- Readable at 20% size: check `out/thumbnail.png` zoomed out.
- **Thumbnail = first 5 seconds:** the hook shows the same number or visual, so the click feels "paid off".
- Upload **3 variants** in Test & Compare (YouTube picks the winner by watch time). Change only one thing per variant (text, color or visual).
- Same layout every time: a recognizable series makes people click *because* it's yours.

## 3. Hooks (first 25 seconds)

Pick one per episode and say it in the first sentence:
1. **Shock number:** "في 0.1 ثانية بس، الناس قرّروا عنك."
2. **Contradiction:** "أقوى شخص في المكان غالبًا مو أقوى واحد."
3. **Direct challenge:** "لو أحد استفزك اليوم، فيه ثلاث ردود… اثنين منها غلط."
4. **Story cold-open:** "سنة 1969، رجل هادي دخل مجلس الشيوخ… وطلع ومعه 20 مليون."
5. **Viewer mirror:** "إذا تحس إن الناس ما تاخذك على محمل الجد… هذا الفيديو لك."
6. **Test:** "قبل ما نبدأ: جاوب على هالسؤال في بالك."
7. **Promise + count:** "ومعنا 8 قصص حقيقية… الخامسة بتصدمك."

## 4. Retention devices already built into the template

| Device | Where | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Progress dots ("story 3 of 8") | top of story scenes | Viewers want to complete the set |
| Globe flight to a new country | each story start | New visual peak every ~35 s (re-engagement) |
| Numbers counting up | story stats | Motion + curiosity |
| Loading skeletons filling in | story card | Anticipation: "what's coming in that box?" |
| Open loop in the hook ("الخامسة بتصدمك") | hook | Gives a reason to stay |
| Takeaway pill | end of each story | A mini reward each time |
| Next-lesson question | outro | Drives session time to the next video |

Look at the **retention graph** every week: a drop at a story means that story or its first line was weak. Swap
story order so the strongest stories sit at positions 1, 3 and the last.

## 5. Shorts: the discovery engine

Make **3 Shorts per episode** (9:16; aim for 30–60 s even though YouTube allows up to 3 min), each standing alone. The studio renders them: `npm run render -- EP00N --shorts`.
1. **Hook + best story:** the strongest number in the first 2 seconds.
2. **"Did you know?" story:** another country, a fast visual.
3. **The exercise or test:** "جرّبها اليوم وقل لي وش صار".

Tips: text on screen in the first frame; no logo intro; a loopable ending (the last line leads back into the first);
always link to the full lesson ("Related video"). Use YouTube's **Shorts series** feature (launched Sept 2026) to
group them as "الموسم 1" so viewers binge in order.

## 6. 2026 platform features to use

| Feature | Use it how |
|---|---|
| **Test & Compare** (titles + thumbnails, up to 3 each) | Every long video |
| **Shorts series** (seasons/episodes, Sept 2026) | One series per season of lessons |
| **Video A/B tests of up to 3 cuts** (announced Sept 2026, coming soon) | Test two hooks for the same episode |
| **Collaborations** (co-creator videos), **Community posts**, **Playlists** | Polls ("وش الدرس الجاي؟"), season playlists |
| **Auto-dubbing / multi-language audio** | Later: an English track to reach global viewers (higher ad rates) |
| **Hype** (for smaller channels) | Ask loyal viewers to Hype new episodes in the first week |

## 7. Comment engine

- The exercise ends with a question ("وش صار؟ كيف حسّيت؟").
- Pin a comment with a **choice question** ("أي قصة أثرت فيك أكثر؟ 1 ولا 7؟"); choices get more replies than open questions.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour; heart the best ones; turn great comments into future Shorts ("ردًا على تعليق…").

## 8. Shareability

People share content that makes **them** look good: wise, kind, funny. Each episode should have one
"forwardable" line (the takeaway) and one surprising number. That's what travels on WhatsApp and X.

## 11. Posting time

Start with **8–9 pm Saudi time** (Mon & Thu), when Gulf evening viewing peaks. After 4 weeks check
YouTube Studio → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube" and move to the darkest slot.
