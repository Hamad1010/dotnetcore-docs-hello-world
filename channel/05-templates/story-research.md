# Story research checklist (5 minutes per story)

A story qualifies only if **all** boxes are ticked.

- [ ] **Real:** reported by ≥1 reputable source (major news outlet, official site, peer-reviewed paper, Guinness, Wikipedia *plus* the primary source it cites). Viral social posts alone don't count.
- [ ] **Teaches this lesson:** in one sentence, how does the story prove the lesson? (If it needs two sentences, pick another story.)
- [ ] **Has numbers:** a year + at least one striking figure (money, time, distance, people, percentage).
- [ ] **Numbers match the source exactly.** If sources disagree → use the safer wording ("about…", "more than…") or drop the number. Note it under "Disputed" in `sources.md`.
- [ ] **Quotes:** exact only if the source quotes it; otherwise paraphrase ("قال إنه ما يستاهل يفوز كذا").
- [ ] **Diversity:** adds a new country/continent/topic to the episode's mix.
- [ ] **Safe:** no politics, no sectarian angle, no mocking any culture, no private individuals, no graphic details.
- [ ] **Story bank:** added to `02-lessons/story-bank.csv` with sources and the lessons it can teach.

## Story shape (30–40 seconds spoken)
1. **Where + when:** «القصة الثالثة من [الدولة]، سنة [السنة].»
2. **The situation** (the tension, in one or two short sentences)
3. **What the person did** (the lesson in action)
4. **The numbers** (show them on screen as `stats`)
5. **The result**
6. **Takeaway:** the lesson again, in new words (≤6 words on screen)

## Good sources to search
News archives (BBC, Reuters, AP, Al Arabiya, Gulf News), Guinness World Records, official company/foundation
pages, university news pages for studies, Wikipedia references (follow them to the original).
