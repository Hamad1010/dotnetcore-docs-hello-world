## Door 1 · Jack Ma's six minutes with Masayoshi Son (2000)
- Jack Ma failed the university entrance exam twice before passing (check); taught English from 1988 to 1993 at what is now
  Hangzhou Dianzi University for about $12 a month; applied for many jobs, including KFC, where 23 of 24 applicants were hired
  and he was not (his own account to Charlie Rose); founded Alibaba in 1999 in his apartment with friends:
  [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.in/thelife/the-unbelievable-and-inspiring-life-story-of-alibaba-founder-jack-ma-the-richest-man-in-china/slidelist/57430193.cms/The) ·
  [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/slideshow/300854) · [Gulf News](https://www.pressreader.com/uae/gulf-news/20180910/281595241422996).
- The meeting: in Ma's 2008 speech it lasted six minutes; after about five minutes Son extended it; Son offered more (reported
  as $40 or $50 million) and Ma declined it as too large; they settled on $20 million. Years later SoftBank's stake was worth
  tens of billions (≈$135 billion at one point, per Reuters):
  [Business Standard / Reuters](https://www.business-standard.com/article/reuters/mega-ipo-to-rekindle-the-bromance-behind-alibaba-s-rise-114082800064_1.html) ·
  [TechNode](https://technode.com/2015/01/23/alibabas-biggest-stockholder-invested-company-1999/) ·
  [SCMP](https://amp.scmp.com/tech/article/3249933/japans-softbank-concludes-run-alibabas-biggest-shareholder-drawing-close-one-most-successful).
  Disputed and left out: the stake percentage, the exact offer before $20 million.
- **Evidence:** Willis and Todorov (Princeton), "First Impressions: Making Up Your Mind After a 100-Ms Exposure to a Face",
  *Psychological Science* 17(7), 592–598, 2006: judgments after 100 ms correlated highly with unhurried ones, most for
  trustworthiness; more time raised confidence more than it changed the judgment; Todorov cautioned that quick impressions can
  be overcome: [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16866745/) ·
  [Princeton](https://princeton.edu/news/2006/08/22/snap-judgments-decide-faces-character-psychologist-finds) ·
  [BPS](https://bps.org.uk/research-digest/your-trustworthiness-judged-tenth-second-or-less).

## Door 3 · Henry Ford's dinner with Thomas Edison (1896)
- Ford was an engineer with the Edison Illuminating Company in Detroit; his first car (the Quadricycle) ran in 1896 (check);
  on 11 August 1896, at the convention of the Association of Edison Illuminating Companies, Oriental Hotel, Manhattan Beach,
  he met Edison at the banquet; Edison encouraged him to keep at it; Ford's own account (*Edison as I Know Him*) has the sketch
  and «Young man, that's the thing; you have it. Keep at it.»; Ford left the Edison company in 1899 and founded Ford Motor
  Company in 1903; a lifelong friendship:
  [The Henry Ford, the friendship](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections-and-research/digital-collections/expert-sets/101111/) ·
  [The Henry Ford, the hotel](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/166230) ·
  [Fortnightly](https://www.fortnightly.com/node/24356) · [Edison as I Know Him](https://www.scribd.com/doc/127738506/Edison-as-I-Know-Him) (check).
  Ford's age: born 30 July 1863 → 33 in August 1896.
- **Evidence:** Fiske, Cuddy and Glick, "Universal dimensions of social cognition: warmth and competence", *Trends in Cognitive
  Sciences* 11, 77–83, 2007: two universal dimensions, with warmth judged first (the "primacy of warmth"):
  [Princeton](https://collaborate.princeton.edu/en/publications/universal-dimensions-of-social-cognition-warmth-and-competence/) ·
  [APS Observer](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/observer/universal-social-cognition).

## Door 4 · Airbnb's founders and the cereal boxes (2008–2009)
- Rejected by investors and in credit-card debt, the founders sold specially designed cereal boxes for $40 each in 2008 and
  raised about $30,000 (sources range from ~800 to ~1,000 boxes); the Y Combinator interview with Paul Graham went badly; a box
  changed his mind; in Brian Chesky's account Graham said, roughly, that people who can get $40 for a box of cereal can get
  strangers to stay in each other's homes; they joined Y Combinator in January 2009; Airbnb is worth tens of billions:
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/04/18/airbnb-ceo-says-he-wooed-first-investors-with-boxes-of-cereal.html) ·
  [Fortune](https://dc.fortune.com/2023/04/19/airbnb-ceo-cereal-box-investors-changed-everything-billion-dollar-company) ·
  [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.in/how-a-box-of-cereal-and-acting-like-a-cockroach-helped-airbnb-become-a-billion-dollar-business/articleshow/21270791.cms).
  The boxes were themed on the 2008 US election; the script and the screen leave the theme out (no politics, CLAUDE.md rule 6).
- **Evidence:** Barrick, Swider and Stewart, "Initial Evaluations in the Interview", *Journal of Applied Psychology* 95(6),
  1163–1172, 2010: impressions in the rapport-building minutes of structured interviews related to the end-of-interview ratings
  (r = .42) and to internship offers (r = .22), and included early judgments of competence beyond liking:
  [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20718533/) · [University of Iowa](https://iro.uiowa.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Initial-Evaluations-in-the-Interview-Relationships/9984380442802771) ·
  [BPS](https://www.bps.org.uk/research-digest/psychologists-have-looked-importance-pre-interview-chitchat).

## Door 5 · Google's founders' first meeting (1995)
- In summer 1995 Sergey Brin was assigned to show Larry Page around Stanford; per the Computer History Museum they argued
  constantly and later became close friends; they worked together on BackRub (1996) and founded Google in 1998:
  [Computer History Museum](https://www.computerhistory.org/revolution/the-web/20/390) ·
  [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/technology/how-google-was-founded) ·
  [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/article/flashback-friday-how-two-stanford-university-whiz-kids-accidentally-built-google/).
  «طالب دكتوراه»: Brin was a PhD student (check).
- **Evidence:** Boothby, Cooney, Sandstrom and Clark, "The Liking Gap in Conversations", *Psychological Science*, 2018 (as in
  EP005): [ScienceDaily](https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2018/09/180910160629.htm); and Todorov's caution (door 1).
