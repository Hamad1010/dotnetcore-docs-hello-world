## The problem

  North Africa 14%): [Gallup](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/705914/state-global-workplace-kuwait-country-level-data.aspx).

  [Gallup](https://courses.gallup.com/strengths-coaching-certification) ·
  [Gallup PDF](https://matsugov.us/docs/general/24330/Clifton-Strengths-Power-of-Strengths.pdf). Correlation, not cause.
