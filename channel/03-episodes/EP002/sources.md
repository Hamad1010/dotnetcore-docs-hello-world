## 3 · Station 1: the problem in numbers
- Gallup, State of the Global Workplace 2026 (2025 data): 20% of employees worldwide engaged (lowest since 2020); low engagement
  costs about $10 trillion in lost productivity; 263,810 respondents in 140+ countries and territories:
  [Gallup](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/697904/state-of-the-global-workplace-2026.aspx).
- Saudi Arabia: 26% engaged (MENA 14%, world 20%):
  [Gallup, Saudi country data](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/706970/state-global-workplace-saudi-arabia-country-level-data.aspx).
- People who use their strengths every day are six times as likely to be engaged:
  [Gallup](https://courses.gallup.com/strengths-coaching-certification) ·
  [Gallup PDF](https://matsugov.us/docs/general/24330/Clifton-Strengths-Power-of-Strengths.pdf). Correlation, not cause.
- Arithmetic: 20% = «من كل 10… اثنين». 26% ≈ a quarter → «تقريبًا 3 من كل 4» not engaged (74%). $10 trillion ÷ 8.2 billion
  people ([UN World Population Prospects 2024](https://www.un.org/development/desa/pd/world-population-prospects-2024)) = $1,220 × 3.75 ≈ 4,570 riyals → «أكثر من 4 آلاف ريال».

## 5 · Station 4: the traps
- **Quitting tomorrow:** Raffiee & Feng (2014), "Should I Quit My Day Job? A Hybrid Path to Entrepreneurship", *Academy of
  Management Journal* 57(4), 936–963, University of Wisconsin–Madison: for people who start while employed, "the hazard of exit
  is 33.3% lower": [paper](https://msbfile03.usc.edu/digitalmeasures/joe.raffiee/intellcont/Raffiee_Feng_2014_AMJ-1.pdF) ·
  [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kimberly-weisul/keep-day-job-nicest-possible-way.html) ·
  [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/article/237755). "Three doors, the job closes one" is our picture of "a third".
  Loai left his job in 2005 (Wamda above); Blakely kept selling fax machines while she spent two years developing Spanx
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Blakely), CNBC above). Fahd (Dammam) is an illustration; "half your salary
  for 6 months" is an example, not a rule.
- **Waiting for passion:** O'Keefe, Dweck & Walton (2018), "Implicit Theories of Interest: Finding Your Passion or Developing
  It?", *Psychological Science* 29(10), 1653–1664: in study 5, people led to a "fixed" view lost interest more once the new
  material became difficult: [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6180666) ·
  [Stanford news](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2018/06/find-passion-may-bad-advice). Lab studies with students.
  Google began in 1996 as BackRub, a research project of two Stanford PhD students:
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Google). Reem (Jeddah) is an illustration; "30 days" is our advice.

## 8 · Part 10 «وقفة»
- **Quran, Az-Zukhruf 43:32** (from «نحن قسمنا»), Uthmani text as in the Madinah mushaf: [Quran.com](https://quran.com/43/32).
  Tafsir: al-Muyassar (King Fahd Complex): «ليكون بعضهم مسخَّرًا لبعض في المعاش»; Ibn Kathir: «ليسخر بعضهم بعضا في الأعمال،
  لاحتياج هذا إلى هذا»; al-Sa'di points to the wisdom in people's differences for work and crafts:
  [surahquran.org (several tafsirs)](https://surahquran.org/aya-tafsser-32-43.html) · [Dorar, tafsir encyclopedia](https://dorar.net/tafseer/43/6).
  «سُخْرِيًّا» here means employing one another in work, not mockery. «موهبتك… هي الشي اللي غيرك محتاجه منك» is our
  application, said after the tafsir, not as tafsir.
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 2262** (Book of Hire), from Abu Hurairah: «مَا بَعَثَ اللَّهُ نَبِيًّا إِلَّا رَعَى الْغَنَمَ»، فَقَالَ
  أَصْحَابُهُ: وَأَنْتَ؟ فَقَالَ: «نَعَمْ، كُنْتُ أَرْعَاهَا عَلَى قَرَارِيطَ لِأَهْلِ مَكَّةَ»:
  [Dorar](https://dorar.net/hadith/sharh/118739) · [HadeethEnc 6219](https://hadeethenc.com/ar/browse/hadith/6219).
  The scholars' explanation (training in caring for and guiding people, patience, gentleness; the humility of the prophets in
  simple work): HadeethEnc above · [Ibn Baz](https://binbaz.org.sa/audios/3622). Disputed: «قراريط» = small wages, or a place in
  Makkah, so the narration doesn't explain the word.
- **Sahih Muslim 2664** (Book of Qadar), from Abu Hurairah; we quote its middle sentence exactly:
  «احْرِصْ عَلَى مَا يَنْفَعُكَ، وَاسْتَعِنْ بِاللَّهِ وَلَا تَعْجَزْ»: [Dorar](https://dorar.net/h/Ov16uqG0) ·
  [IslamOnline (sharh)](https://fiqh.islamonline.net/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D8%AD-%D8%AD%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AB-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A4%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D9%88%D9%8A/).
