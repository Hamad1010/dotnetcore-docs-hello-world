# EP009 · الملف 09: سر الجسر · Sources

Checked 10 Nov 2026 for the v6 draft. Web search only; links marked "check" should be opened once before publishing.

## Door 1 · David Cheriton's porch (1998)
- In August 1998 Stanford professor David Cheriton suggested that Larry Page and Sergey Brin meet Andy Bechtolsheim (a Sun
  Microsystems co-founder); they met on Cheriton's front porch in Palo Alto; Bechtolsheim wrote a $100,000 cheque to "Google
  Inc." before the company was incorporated (the next month); Cheriton invested too (sources differ on his amount: $100,000,
  $200,000 or $250,000, so the script gives none); he is among the richest university professors ($10 billion, per Benzinga):
  [Inc. (Walter Isaacson)](https://www.inc.com/walter-isaacson/how-google-got-its-start.html) ·
  [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/news/23/05/32475257/the-professor-who-made-10-billion-by-cutting-google-its-first-startup-check) ·
  Wikipedia "Andy Bechtolsheim" and "David Cheriton" (check).
- Cheriton and Bechtolsheim founded Granite Systems in 1995 and sold it to Cisco in September 1996 for about $220 million in
  stock: [Cisco newsroom](https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y1996/m09/cisco-systems-to-acquire-granite-systems.html).
  «قبلها بثلاث سنين… وباعوها بعد سنة تقريبًا»: 1995 → 1998 = 3; 1995 → 1996 ≈ 1.
- **Evidence:** Burt (University of Chicago), "Structural Holes and Good Ideas", *American Journal of Sociology* 110(2), 349–399,
  2004: 673 managers in the supply chain of a large American electronics company (reported as Raytheon); ideas were rated by
  senior judges; brokers across groups had their ideas judged more valuable and were ahead in compensation, evaluations and
  promotions: [Stanford reprint](https://snap.stanford.edu/class/cs224w-readings/Burt04StructureHole.pdf) ·
  [blog on the NYT report](https://connectedness.blogspot.com/2004/07/good-ideas-at-raytheon-and-big-holes.html).

## Door 2 · Reid Hoffman's "most expensive decision" (2004)
- Sean Parker asked Hoffman to lead Facebook's first round; Hoffman declined because of a conflict with his role at LinkedIn,
  set up the meeting between Mark Zuckerberg and Peter Thiel (per David Kirkpatrick's *The Facebook Effect*), and invested
  alongside; he called not leading it "the most expensive decision" of his business career:
  [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/reid-hoffman) · [Academy of Achievement](https://achievement.org/achiever/reid-hoffman/) ·
  [Wired archive](https://wirednews.pages.dev/story/reid-hoffman-network-philosopher).
- Thiel invested $500,000 for 10.2% in August 2004, the first outside investor; most sources put his total proceeds at about
  $1 billion: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/id/47386291) · Wikipedia "Peter Thiel" (check).
  «شون باركر… اللي كان يساعد في إدارتها»: he was Facebook's president in 2004 (check).
- **Evidence:** Nielsen, Trust in Advertising 2021: more than 40,000 online respondents in Latin America, North America, Europe,
  the Middle East and Africa, and Asia-Pacific; 88% trust recommendations from people they know, the most of any channel:
  [Nielsen](https://www.nielsen.com/insights/2021/beyond-martech-building-trust-with-consumers-and-engaging-where-sentiment-is-high/) ·
  [sell sheet](https://journal.tinkoff.ru/media/2021-nielsen-trust-in-advertising-sell-sheet.axsmh1vlgips..pdf).

## Door 3 · Oprah Winfrey and Phil McGraw
- After an April 1996 episode on mad cow disease, Texas cattlemen sued Winfrey (the Amarillo beef trial, 1998); McGraw's trial
  consulting helped her defence; she won; she invited him on her show in April 1998; he became a weekly regular; *Dr. Phil*
  premiered in September 2002, created with Winfrey's Harpo Productions, which co-produced it until 2010 (accounts differ on
  Harpo's exact role): [CNN Money (Fortune)](https://money.cnn.com/galleries/2010/news/1009/gallery.oprah_spinoffs.fortune/index.html) ·
  [Next TV](https://nexttv.com/news/dr-phil-mcgraw-145078) · [Time](https://content.time.com/time/specials/packages/article/0,28804,1944527_1944528_1944538,00.html) ·
  [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/print/article/1016703) (check). Her "burger" line is from the 1996 episode (check).
- **Evidence:** Uzzi (Northwestern) and Spiro, "Collaboration and Creativity: The Small World Problem", *American Journal of
  Sociology*, 2005: 2,092 people in 474 Broadway musicals, 1945–1989; performance (artistic and financial) rose with a mix of
  repeat collaborators and newcomers, up to a threshold, then fell:
  [Kellogg Insight](https://insight.kellogg.northwestern.edu/article/collaboration_and_creativity) ·
  [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2005/04/050428172755.htm).
