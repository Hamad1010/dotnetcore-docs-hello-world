## 1 · Hook: Careem and Uber (2012 → 2019)
- **The start.** Careem began in Dubai in July 2012 (some sources say June); its founders Mudassir Sheikha and Magnus Olsson were
  McKinsey consultants who travelled for work and found car bookings unreliable; it started as a website for corporate car
  bookings and only later became an app: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Careem) ·
  [MENAbytes](https://www.menabytes.com/how-careem-started-infographic/) ·
  [Careem engineering blog](https://engineering.careem.com/posts/a-decade-in-motion-the-story-being-the-regions-first-tech-unicorn).
  Sheikha is an American of Pakistani origin, Olsson is Swedish (same Wikipedia page). We name the consulting firm only as «شركة
  استشارات عالمية».
- **The name.** «كريم» from generosity; the founders wanted to be generous to customers, captains and colleagues:
  [McKinsey interview](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/technology-media-and-telecommunications/our-insights/careems-co-founders-on-finding-purpose-building-a-unicorn) ·
  [Harvard RC-TOM](https://d3.harvard.edu/platform-rctom/submission/careem-why-is-a-local-startup-winning-the-competition-with-a-global-giant-like-uber).
  Careem calls its drivers «كباتن» (captains): Wikipedia above. We don't say when the word was first used.
- **Uber arrives.** Uber's own blog (26 Aug 2013): Dubai was "our first market in the Middle East":
  [Uber](https://www.uber.com/ae/en/blog/dubai-just-went-uber/) · [TNW, 27 Aug 2013](https://thenextweb.com/news/ubers-taxi-service-arrives-in-dubai).
  Careem started in July 2012, so Uber came in its second year («من ثاني سنة»… «وبعدها بسنة»). The fuller service followed in
  September 2013 (Gulf Business), which doesn't change the year.
- **The money in 2013.** Google Ventures put about $258 million into Uber, announced 22 Aug 2013 (four days before Dubai), part of a
  $361 million round: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2013/08/22/google-ventures-puts-258m-into-uber-its-largest-deal-ever/) ·
  [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2013/08/23/more-details-on-ubers-massive-funding-round). Careem's 2013 round: $1.7 million, led by
  STC Ventures: [Global Venturing (2013)](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2013/09/10/careem-picks-up-saudi-telecom-investment) ·
  [Global Venturing (2014)](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2014/02/13/stc-chauffeurs-1-7m-deal) · Wikipedia above.
  Arithmetic: 258 ÷ 1.7 = 151.8 → «بأكثر من 150 مرة» (Google's part alone against Careem's whole round, so the real gap was larger).
- **What they did.** Olsson: the company began as an effort to "find a local problem, and solve that local problem"; Careem built
  its own mapping system because addresses and maps in the region were less reliable:
  [CNN (2017)](https://edition.cnn.com/2017/03/01/middleeast/careem-dubai-startup). Riders could pay cash, unlike Uber's initial
  card-only system; in countries like Egypt and Pakistan most people pay cash:
  [AP via Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/markets/uber-acquires-mideast-competitor-careem-for-3-1-billion).
  «جنب المسجد… لف يمين عند البقالة» is our everyday picture of the address problem, not a quote.
- **«عنواني».** January 2015: Careem bought Enwani, a Saudi-based start-up launched in 2011 by Saudi and German entrepreneurs to solve
  the postal-address problem in Saudi Arabia; its co-founder Abdulla Elyas joined Careem as a co-founder:
  [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2015/01/careem-acquires-saudi-based-home-delivery-service-enwani) ·
  [Wamda (Arabic)](https://www.wamda.com/ar/2015/01/%E2%80%98%D9%83%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%85%E2%80%98-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D8%B0-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-%E2%80%98%D8%B9%D9%86%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%E2%80%98) ·
  [WEF profile](https://weforum.org/people/abdulla-elyas). Disputed: the WEF profile says 2014; Wamda and Gulf Business say January
  2015 (we say 2015). His nationality isn't stated in these sources, so we don't name him or his nationality.
- **Saudi Arabia, the biggest market.** 2016: "Saudi Arabia – its largest market":
  [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2016/transport/dubais-careem-launches-operations-turkey-set-expand-saudi);
  2018: [Bloomberg Businessweek](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2018-02-02/what-it-s-like-to-be-the-uber-of-the-middle-east).
  By 2022 Pakistan had more cumulative rides (299M vs 242M; [Arab News](https://arabnews.pk/node/2208296)), so we say «صارت»
  (it became), about those years, not "is".
- **The deal.** 26 March 2019: Uber agreed to buy Careem for $3.1 billion ($1.4B cash + $1.7B convertible notes); Careem kept its
  brand; closed in January 2020: [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2019/03/uber-acquires-careem-3-1-billion) ·
  [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2019/middle-east/uber-expected-buy-careem-week-3-1bn) ·
  [Business Wire (close)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200102005557/en/Uber-Completes-Acquisition-Careem).
  Users: 33 million (Wamda) or "more than 30 million" ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/cars/2019/03/26/uber-acquires-careem-for-3-1-billion-to-dominate-ride-hailing-in-the-middle-east/))
  → «أكثر من 30 مليون مستخدم»; 15 countries, 120 cities.
- **Uber in 2019:** 63 countries, 700+ cities (IPO filing): [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1543151/000095012319002820/filename1.htm) ·
  [ITWeb](https://itweb.co.za/article/uber-raises-81bn-as-it-makes-stock-market-debut/KA3Ww7dlk9r7rydZ) → «أكثر من 60 دولة».
- Arithmetic: $3.1B × 3.75 = 11.6 billion riyals → «أكثر من 11 مليار ريال». 3,100 ÷ 1.7 = 1,824 → «بأكثر من 1800 مرة» (the deal
  against the 2013 round). «العملاق ما قدر ياخذ مكانهم… فاشتراه» is our reading of the story, said as a punchline, not a quote.

## 8 · Part 10 «وقفة»
- **Quran, al-Qasas 28:26**, text as in the Madinah mushaf: [King Saud University Quran](https://quran.ksu.edu.sa/tafseer/katheer/sura28-aya26.html) ·
  [Quranpedia](https://quranpedia.net/note/6096). Before it, 28:23–24 (he watered for the two women); after it, 28:27–28 (her father
  offered him work for eight years and he agreed) → «وأبوها… اتفق معه يشتغل عنده».
  Tafsir: al-Muyassar (hire him to tend the flock; the best hire is strong in the work and trustworthy, one whose betrayal you don't
  fear) and al-Sa'di (strength and trustworthiness are what to look for in anyone who works for you; the work fails when one is
  missing): [surahquran.org (several tafsirs)](https://surahquran.org/aya-tafsser-26-28.html) · [Dorar, tafsir encyclopedia](https://dorar.net/tafseer/28/6).
  Not used: the details of how she knew (the rock, walking behind him), which come from narrations quoted by Ibn Kathir, and the
  father's name, on which the tafsirs differ.
- **Sahih al-Bukhari 4380** (كتاب المغازي، باب قصة أهل نجران), from Hudhayfa: the people of Najran asked for a trustworthy man;
  «لَأَبْعَثَنَّ مَعَكُمْ رَجُلًا أَمِينًا حَقَّ أَمِينٍ»، فَاسْتَشْرَفَ لَهُ أَصْحَابُ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ، فَقَالَ: «قُمْ يَا أَبَا عُبَيْدَةَ بْنَ الْجَرَّاحِ»،
  فَلَمَّا قَامَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: «هَذَا أَمِينُ هَذِهِ الْأُمَّةِ»: [Dorar](https://dorar.net/h/xpLIf2r7) ·
  [Dorar, sharh](https://dorar.net/hadith/sharh/150388). Muslim (2420) tells the same story with «إليكم» and without the last two
  sentences, so the narration says «رواه البخاري». We leave out the first half of the hadith (why the delegation came).
  «فاستشرف له» = they looked up to it, each hoping to be the one described (Dorar's sharh) → «وتطلّع لها الصحابة… كل واحد يتمنى يكون هو».
- **Ibn Hajar, Fath al-Bari 7/93:** trustworthiness was shared by the companions, but the Prophet ﷺ singled some out with qualities
  that were strongest in them: [al-Maktaba al-Shamela](https://shamela.ws/book/232/1018) → «الأمانة كانت في الصحابة كلهم… بس أبو عبيدة
  كانت فيه أكثر… فصارت صفته». We don't use his examples for other companions. **Check the page before publishing.**
- «صفتك ما تبنيها بإعلان… تبنيها بفعلك» is our application, said after the meaning, not as tafsir.
- On screen: the verse and the hadith as written, with their references; no sound effects; the hum fades out; no picture of any
  prophet or companion (only text, water, sheep, desert).
