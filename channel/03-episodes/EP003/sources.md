## Hook: Careem and Uber (2012 → 2019)
- Careem founded in 2012 by Magnus Olsson and Mudassir Sheikha, two former McKinsey consultants; Uber agreed in March
  2019 to buy it for $3.1 billion ($1.4B cash + $1.7B convertible notes); Careem kept its brand and app:
  [AP via Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/markets/uber-acquires-mideast-competitor-careem-for-3-1-billion) ·
  [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2019/03/uber-acquires-careem-3-1-billion) ·
  [CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/news/business/uber-acquires-mideast-competitor-careem-1.5071635)
- AP called it the largest technology deal in the Middle East outside Israel. We say "من أكبر صفقات التقنية في تاريخ
  المنطقة" (one of the largest) and keep politics out.
- Cash: Careem let riders pay cash, unlike Uber's initial credit-card-only system; in countries like Egypt and
  Pakistan most people pay cash (same AP report).
- Arithmetic: $3.1B × 3.75 = 11.6 billion riyals → "أكثر من 11 مليار ريال".
