## 1 · Hook: Abdul Latif Jameel and Toyota (1945–2025)
- **1945, a petrol station in Jeddah.** The business began in 1945 when Abdul Latif Jameel ran a petrol station on the old
  Makkah road in Jeddah: [Al Arabiya (2016)](https://english.alarabiya.net/business/aviation-and-transport/2016/09/02/Abdul-Latif-Jameel-A-Saudi-Japanese-success-story) ·
  [Abdul Latif Jameel Enterprises](https://alj-enterprises.com/about/) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Latif_Jameel).
  1955 − 1945 = 10 → «بعد عشر سنين».
- **Four cars, little money.** "After growing fond of the early Land Cruiser… he placed an order for four units and was
  subsequently appointed Toyota's sole distributor in the kingdom"; the initial order was four Toyota BJ all-terrain vehicles,
  the Land Cruiser's precursor: [Jameel Motors, our story](https://jameelmotors.com/en/our-story/) ·
  [Family Business Histories](https://familybusinesshistories.org/spotlights/abdul-latif-jameel/) (the financial constraints).
- **October 1955.** Toyota's own history: exports to Saudi Arabia began in April 1955 with one Land Cruiser; the distributor
  agreement with Abdul Latif Jameel was signed in October 1955:
  [Toyota, 75 years](https://www.toyota-global.com/company/history_of_toyota/75years/data/automotive_business/sales/activity/middle_east/index.html).
- **Desert roads.** The company describes its early Toyota 4×4s as "the perfect antidote to the Kingdom's dusty desert roads"
  ([ALJ, 80 years](https://alj.com/en/perspective/abdul-latif-jameel-80-years-of-inspiring-change/)): a company claim, told as such.
- **Instalments.** The company says it was "one of the first to pioneer installment-based sales" in Saudi Arabia (ALJ, 80
  years, above). Disputed date: the early 1960s there, the late 1970s on [Jameel Motors](https://jameelmotors.com/en/financial-services/)
  → the script gives no date («من أوائل اللي بدوا البيع بالتقسيط»).
- **The 1980s.** "By the 1980s, ALJ was importing and selling over 250,000 Toyota vehicles per year": Al Arabiya above.
  525,600 minutes a year ÷ 250,000 = 2.1 → «تقريبًا سيارة… كل دقيقتين».
- **Lexus 1989; 70 years in 2025:** Al Arabiya above ·
  [Toyota Saudi Arabia, 70 years](https://www.toyota.com.sa/en/news/70-years-of-success) ·
  [ALJ, 70 years](https://alj.com/en/news/abdul-latif-jameel-motors-and-toyota-motor-corporation-celebrate-70-years-of-success-with-landmark-event/).
- Part 1b («كل طرف كان يكسب») is our reading of these facts, not a quote.

## 3 · Who brings the opportunity?
- **Granovetter.** Mark Granovetter's study of people who found jobs through personal contacts (the 1973 article "The
  Strength of Weak Ties", American Journal of Sociology; the 1974 book *Getting a Job*): of 54 people, 16.7% saw the contact
  often, 55.6% occasionally, 27.8% rarely → 83.4% → «أكثر من 8 من كل 10»:
  [Wikipedia, interpersonal ties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interpersonal_ties) ·
  [PMI](https://www.pmi.org/learning/library/viewpoints-career-opportunities-networking-3266). The data were gathered around
  1970 → «في السبعينات». 2022 − 1973 = 49 → «قبل قرابة خمسين سنة».
- **LinkedIn, 2022.** Rajkumar, Saint-Jacques, Bojinov (Harvard), Brynjolfsson (Stanford) and Aral (MIT), "A causal test of the
  strength of weak ties", *Science* 2022: about 20 million users, about 2 billion new ties, 600,000 jobs, five years
  (2015–2019); moderately weak ties helped most:
  [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36107999/) · [MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/2022/weak-ties-linkedin-employment-0915) ·
  [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/a-massive-linkedin-study-reveals-who-actually-helps-you-get-that-job/).
  Saudi Arabia: 32.2 million in the 2022 census ([Asharq Al-Awsat](https://english.aawsat.com/gulf/4358341-saudi-population-32-million-51-under-30));
  20 ÷ 32.2 = 62% → «أكثر من نص سكان السعودية».

## 5 · The four steps
- **Step 1, the five-minute favor.** Adam Rifkin, a software engineer named Fortune's best networker in 2011; his rule: do
  anything that takes five minutes or less for anybody (Adam Grant, *Give and Take*, 2013):
  [The Ladders](https://www.theladders.com/career-advice/how-a-shy-star-trek-loving-software-engineer-became-fortunes-best-networker) ·
  [Big Think](https://bigthink.com/videos/share-your-time-like-a-micro-loan/).
- **Step 1, reciprocity.** Regan (1971), *Journal of Experimental Social Psychology* 7: participants given an unasked-for
  soft drink later bought about twice as many raffle tickets (means reported as 1.73 against 0.92 with no favor):
  [summary](https://www.suebehaviouraldesign.com/en/blog/reciprocity-principle-at-work/). The university is disputed (Cornell /
  Stanford) → «تجربة أمريكية مشهورة». 1.73 ÷ 0.92 = 1.9 → «تقريبًا ضعف».
- **Step 2, the liking gap.** Boothby, Cooney, Sandstrom and Clark (Cornell, Harvard, Essex, Yale), *Psychological Science*
  2018: after conversations people underestimate how much their partners liked them:
  [ScienceDaily](https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2018/09/180910160629.htm) ·
  [paper](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/The-Liking-Gap-in-Conversations.pdf).
- **Step 2, thank-you letters.** Kumar and Epley, *Psychological Science* 2018: writers overestimated how awkward recipients
  would feel and underestimated how positive they would feel:
  [UChicago News](https://news.uchicago.edu/story/people-underestimate-value-sending-letters-appreciation) ·
  [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2018/08/180828172046.htm).
- **Step 3, dormant ties.** Levin, Walter and Murnighan, "Dormant Ties: The Value of Reconnecting", *Organization Science*
  22(4), 2011: executives who reconnected with contacts they had lost touch with got advice that compared favorably with their
  current ties': [Kellogg](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/faculty/research/detail/2011/dormant-ties-the-value-of-reconnecting/) ·
  [RePEc](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:inm:ororsc:v:22:y:2011:i:4:p:923-939). «من سنين»: the paper's dormant ties were
  out of touch for years; we don't give a number.
- **Step 4, Steve Jobs and Bill Hewlett.** At 12 (some sources say 13), Jobs found Hewlett's number in the phone book, asked
  for parts for a frequency counter, got them and a summer job on HP's assembly line; "I've never found anyone who said no or
  hung up the phone when I called. I just asked." (1994 interview):
  [Fortune (2025)](https://fortune.com/2025/03/16/steve-jobs-teenager-hp-cofounder-career-success/) ·
  [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/business-insider/the-gutsy-way-12-year-old-steve-jobs-landed-a-summer-job-at-hp.html) ·
  [Assembly Magazine](https://www.assemblymag.com/articles/94427-in-archived-interview-steve-jobs-recalls-working-on-hp-assembly-line).
  Disputed age → «12… أو 13 سنة»; the map says «ما تعدى عمره 13 سنة».

## 6 · The traps
- **Trap 1, networking only for gain.** Casciaro (Toronto), Gino (Harvard) and Kouchaki (Northwestern), "The Contaminating
  Effects of Building Instrumental Ties: How Networking Can Make Us Feel Dirty", *Administrative Science Quarterly* 59(4), 2014:
  three experiments and a survey of lawyers; instrumental networking made people feel dirty and want cleansing (products such
  as soap), and those who felt it networked less:
  [HBS Working Knowledge](https://hbswk.hbs.edu/item/the-contaminating-effects-of-building-instrumental-ties-how-networking-can-make-us-feel-dirty) ·
  [Harvard DASH](https://dash.harvard.edu/handle/1/13244609) · [Kellogg Insight](https://insight.kellogg.northwestern.edu/article/networking-is-a-dirty-business).
  "Dig Your Well Before You're Thirsty" is Harvey Mackay's book title (1997) → «احفر بيرك… قبل ما تعطش».
- **Trap 2, Theranos.** Board members included former secretaries of state Henry Kissinger and George Shultz, former defense
  secretary William Perry and General James Mattis; valued at $9 billion in 2015; collapsed in 2018; its founder was sentenced
  in November 2022 to 135 months: [Yahoo / The Conversation](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/elizabeth-holmes-trial-politics-celebrity-100359739.html) ·
  [CBS Austin / AP](https://cbsaustin.com/news/nation-world/holmes-gets-more-than-11-years-in-prison-for-theranos-scam).
  9 × 3.75 = 33.75 → «قرابة 34 مليار ريال»; 135 months = 11 years 3 months → «أكثر من 11 سنة». On screen she gets the
  watercolor avatar, never a photo (the owner's rule).
- Fahd (Riyadh), Nayef (Dammam), Majed (Abha) and Saad are illustrations, labelled «مثال توضيحي» on screen.
