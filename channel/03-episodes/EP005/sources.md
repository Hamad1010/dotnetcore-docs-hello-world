## Hook: Abdul Latif Jameel and Toyota (1955 →)
- Toyota's own history: exports to Saudi Arabia began in April 1955 with one Land Cruiser; Abdul Latif Jameel became
  the official distributor in October 1955:
  [Toyota](https://www.toyota-global.com/company/history_of_toyota/75years/data/automotive_business/sales/activity/middle_east/index.html)
- First year: ALJ's history says the first order was four vehicles; another account says ten, five sold in the first
  year → "سيارات تنعد على أصابع اليد": [ALJ](https://alj.com/en/?p=61964) ·
  [CEOWORLD](https://ceoworld.biz/?p=262727)
- By the 1980s ALJ was selling more than 250,000 Toyotas a year, and it still holds the distribution:
  [Al Arabiya (2016)](https://english.alarabiya.net/business/aviation-and-transport/2016/09/02/Abdul-Latif-Jameel-A-Saudi-Japanese-success-story)
- Arithmetic: 250,000 ÷ 365 ≈ 685 a day → "تقريبًا 700"; 1,440 minutes ÷ 685 ≈ 2.1 → "سيارة كل دقيقتين".
  1955 → 2016 (the article) = 61 years → "أكثر من 60 سنة".

## The problem
- Granovetter, "The Strength of Weak Ties" (1973): of the job changers who found work through personal contacts (54
  people), 16.7% saw the contact often, 55.6% occasionally, 27.8% rarely:
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interpersonal_ties) · [AcaWiki](https://acawiki.org/The_strength_of_weak_ties).
  55.6 + 27.8 = 83.4% ≈ 5 of 6.
- Rajkumar, Saint-Jacques, Bojinov, Brynjolfsson and Aral, *Science* (15 Sept 2022): LinkedIn experiments on more than
  20 million people over 5 years, 2 billion new ties and 600,000 new jobs; moderately weak ties helped job moves most,
  and the weakest ties helped less:
  [MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/2022/weak-ties-linkedin-employment-0915) ·
  [Stanford Digital Economy Lab](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/publication/a-causal-test-of-the-strength-of-weak-ties)
- Arithmetic: 20 million ÷ 32.2 million (Saudi census 2022) ≈ 62% → "أكثر من نص سكان السعودية".
