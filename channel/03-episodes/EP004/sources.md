# EP004 · الثقة عملة · Sources

## Hook: Rose Blumkin («مسز بي») and Warren Buffett
- Arrived in the US in 1915 unable to read or speak English; worked every day until 103 (Buffett, 2020 Berkshire
  report): [BizNews](https://www.biznews.com/news/rose-blumkin-make-difference)
- Founded Nebraska Furniture Mart in 1937 with about $500 borrowed from her brother:
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebraska_Furniture_Mart) ·
  [Rose Blumkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose_Blumkin)
- 1983: Berkshire bought most of the store on a handshake; Buffett asked "Mrs. B, do you owe any money?", she said
  no; "We did not get an audit":
  [The Investor's Podcast](https://www.theinvestorspodcast.com/newsletter/the-legend-of-mrs-b/) ·
  [Oh My Omaha](https://ohmyomaha.com/nebraska-furniture-mart/)
- Disputed: the price is $55 million in some sources and $60 million in others (90% of the company) → "أكثر من 50
  مليون دولار" and "أغلب محلها". 1983 − 1937 = 46 years.
- Her motto, "sell cheap, tell the truth, don't cheat nobody" (wording varies):
  [Dallas News](https://dallasnews.com/business/retail/2015/04/09/blame-it-on-mrs-b) ·
  [Focus on the Family](https://dailycitizen.focusonthefamily.com/mrs-b-the-russian-immigrant-who-sang-god-bless-america-every-night-before-dinner/)

## The problem
- Edelman Trust Barometer special report *In Brands We Trust?* (2019): 81% say "I must be able to trust the brand to
  do what is right" is a deciding factor; 16,000 people in 8 markets:
  [Edelman](https://www.edelman.com/insights/trust-barometer-special-report-in-brands-we-trust). 81% → "8 من كل 10".
- Spiegel Research Center (Northwestern): products with 5+ reviews are 270% more likely to be bought than products
  with none; purchase likelihood peaks around 4.2–4.5 stars and a 4.2 beats a perfect 5:
  [Practical Ecommerce](https://www.practicalecommerce.com/Study-5-Star-Reviews-Not-Necessarily-Helpful) ·
  [PowerReviews](https://www.powerreviews.com/the-power-of-negative-reviews/) ·
  [Lipscore](https://lipscore.com/blog/product-reviews-increase-your-online-sales-by-270/).
  270% more = 3.7 times → "تقريبًا أربع أضعاف". Some summaries say 4.0–4.7; we use the more common 4.2–4.5.

## Tools
- Domino's "Pizza Turnaround" (December 2009): ads with real customer complaints ("crust like cardboard"), new
  recipe; Q1 2010 same-store sales +14.3%, an industry record:
  [Columbia Business School](https://business.columbia.edu/insights/brand-talk/dominos-wins-eating-little-humble-pie) ·
  [Campaign](https://www.campaignlive.co.uk/article/case-study-dominos-crispin-porter-bogusky-transformed-pizza-chain-tech-company/1422647)
- Buffett's memo to Berkshire managers, every two years for more than 25 years: "We can afford to lose money, even a
  lot of money. But we can't afford to lose reputation, even a shred of reputation":
  [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/general/2015/01/09/the-3-things-warren-buffett-requested-from-his-all.aspx) ·
  [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/08/06/one-page-memo-billionaire-warren-buffett-sent-to-berkshire-hathaway-managers-every-two-years-for-over-25-years-reputation-over-profit)
- His credentials: on 28 August 2024 Berkshire Hathaway became the first US company outside tech worth $1 trillion:
  [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/buffetts-berkshire-hathaway-hits-1-trillion-market-value-first-us-comp-rcna168601)

## Risks
- Buffett: "You can lose a reputation that took 37 years to build in 37 seconds":
  [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna15212017)
- Tylenol, 1982: after a criminal poisoned capsules, Johnson & Johnson recalled 31 million bottles (about $100 million);
  its share fell from 37% to 7% and came back to about 70% of its old share within five months:
  [Retail Brew](https://www.retailbrew.com/stories/2022/10/13/total-recall-how-the-tylenol-murders-of-1982-ushered-in-tamper-resistant-packaging) ·
  [case study](https://www.communiquepr.com/the-tylenol-murders-a-case-study/).
  Some sources give 35% before the crisis; "recovered within a year" is not supported, so we say five months, 70%.
