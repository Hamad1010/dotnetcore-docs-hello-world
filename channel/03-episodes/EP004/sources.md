## 1 · Hook: Rose Blumkin («مسز بي») and Warren Buffett
- **Arrival.** Born 3 December 1893; came to the US in 1917 and reached Seattle unable to speak English:
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose_Blumkin). Her route in her own oral history: Siberia, China, Japan, then
  Seattle; "I couldn't talk English": [Home Furnishings Hall of Fame](https://www.homefurnishingshalloffame.com/rose-gorelick-blumkin-nebraska-furniture-mart).
  A 1917 passenger manifest from Japan to the US: [Nebraska schools history page](https://ne50000695.schoolwires.net/Page/1884).
  Age: 1917 − 1893 = 23 until her December birthday → «عمرها 23 سنة». We drop the train, the border guard and the "peanut boat"
  that popular retellings add (not in these sources). «لا قراءة ولا كلام»: she never learned to read English (Buffett:
  [BizNews](https://www.biznews.com/news/rose-blumkin-make-difference)). Disputed: BizNews gives 1915 for her arrival;
  Wikipedia and the 1917 manifest give 1917, which we use.
- **The store.** 1937, about $500 borrowed from her brother, in the basement of her husband's store in Omaha; named Nebraska
  Furniture Mart: [Wikipedia (NFM)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebraska_Furniture_Mart) · Wikipedia (Rose Blumkin) above.
  Some pages give 1933 for her first sales; 1937 is the founding year in NFM's page and in Buffett's letters.
- **Sued for selling too cheap.** Carpet suppliers sued her under the Fair Trade laws for selling so low:
  [WFCA Hall of Fame](https://wfca.org/?p=993). Buffett: she won all the cases, and at the end of one she sold the judge $1,400
  of carpet ([Berkshire letters, as quoted](https://www.goodreads.com/notes/18775724-berkshire-hathaway-letters-to-shareholders/15731945-simon-stock));
  her own telling: [Focus on the Family](https://dailycitizen.focusonthefamily.com/mrs-b-the-russian-immigrant-who-sang-god-bless-america-every-night-before-dinner/).
  No court record found, so we tell it as she and Buffett did and leave out who sued and the $1,400.
- **Buffett's credentials.** On 28 August 2024 Berkshire Hathaway became the first US company outside tech worth $1 trillion:
  [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/buffetts-berkshire-hathaway-hits-1-trillion-market-value-first-us-comp-rcna168601).
- **The deal (1983).** Buffett walked in on his birthday, 30 August 1983, with a purchase proposal "just over a page"; she accepted
  it without changing a word, with no investment bankers or lawyers; "Though the company's financial statements were unaudited, I
  had no worries. Mrs. B simply told me what was what, and her word was good enough for me" (his 2013 letter):
  [Furniture Today](https://www.furnituretoday.com/business-news/warren-buffett-brags-about-nebraska-furniture-mart) ·
  [Home News Now](https://homenewsnow.com/?p=2145) ·
  [Washington Times / AP](https://m.washingtontimes.com/news/2014/mar/1/buffett-speaks-highlights-from-his-annual-letter/) ·
  the letter itself: [berkshirehathaway.com](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2013ltr.pdf) (check).
  Age: Aug 1983 − Dec 1893 = 89 → «عمرها 89 سنة». 2013 − 1983 = 30 → «بعد 30 سنة». 1983 − 1937 = 46 → «46 سنة».
- **The two questions.** He asked whether she owned the building and whether she owed any money; yes and no, and that was all he
  needed. He also said the purchase was made without any audit of the books or the inventory, "absolutely unheard of" in business:
  [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/z/41629447) · [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/warren-buffett-bought-nebraska-furniture-233107796.html) ·
  [The Investor's Podcast](https://www.theinvestorspodcast.com/newsletter/the-legend-of-mrs-b/) ("Mrs. B, do you owe any money?").
  EP003's teaser said «سألها سؤال»; it doesn't say "only one", so the two questions keep its promise.
- **Disputed: the price and the stake.** $55 million in some sources, $60 million in others; 90% in most, 80% in a few:
  Wikipedia (NFM) and the links above → «أكثر من 50 مليون دولار» and «أغلب محلها».
  Riyals: 50 × 3.75 = 187.5, 55 × 3.75 = 206, 60 × 3.75 = 225 → «قرابة 200 مليون ريال» holds for every figure.
- **The 1983 letter.** "I'd rather wrestle grizzlies than compete with Mrs. B and her progeny"; "Mrs. B, now 90", still on the
  sales floor seven days a week: [the 1983 letter (copy)](https://www.scribd.com/document/607532415/Chairman-s-Letter-1983) ·
  [Fortune classics, 1983](https://fortune.com/article/letters-from-chairman-buffett-fortune-classics-1983) ·
  [AOL](https://www.aol.com/news/warren-buffett-said-89-old-135144152.html) ·
  the original: [berkshirehathaway.com](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1983.html) (check).
- **103.** She worked until she was 103: Wikipedia (Rose Blumkin) · BizNews above.
- **Her motto**, "sell cheap, tell the truth, don't cheat nobody" (the wording varies a little):
  [Dallas News](https://dallasnews.com/business/retail/2015/04/09/blame-it-on-mrs-b) · Focus on the Family above.

## 3 · كم تسوى الثقة؟
- **Edelman (2019).** Special report *In Brands We Trust?*: 81% say "I must be able to trust the brand to do what is right" is a
  deciding factor; 16,000 people in 8 markets:
  [Edelman](https://www.edelman.com/insights/trust-barometer-special-report-in-brands-we-trust). 81% → «من كل 10… 8».
- **PwC (2024), Saudi Arabia.** Norma Taki, PwC Middle East's consumer markets leader: 62% of Saudi consumers prefer to check
  online reviews before buying, against a global average of 52%:
  [Argaam, 22 Feb 2024](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1707303). PwC's earlier Pulse 6 survey gave 58% for
  KSA ([PwC](https://www.pwc.com/m1/en/media-centre/articles/global-consumer-insights-survey-pulse-6-ksa-perspective.html)); we
  use the 2024 figure with its year. 62% → «6 من كل 10» in the alternative hook.
- **Spiegel Research Center (Northwestern).** Products with 5+ reviews are 270% more likely to be bought than products with none:
  [Lipscore](https://lipscore.com/blog/product-reviews-increase-your-online-sales-by-270/) ·
  [PowerReviews](https://www.powerreviews.com/the-power-of-negative-reviews/). 270% more = 3.7 times → «تقريبًا 4 أضعاف».

## 5 · The four steps
- **Step 1.** «قل موعد التسليم… وسلّم قبله بيوم» is our advice; Noura's gift boxes are an illustration, labelled
  [مثال توضيحي] on screen.
- **Step 2, Domino's.** December 2009 "Pizza Turnaround": ads with real customers' complaints ("crust like cardboard") and a new
  recipe; US same-store sales +14.3% in Q1 2010, called an industry record:
  [Columbia Business School](https://business.columbia.edu/insights/brand-talk/dominos-wins-eating-little-humble-pie) ·
  [Campaign](https://www.campaignlive.co.uk/article/case-study-dominos-crispin-porter-bogusky-transformed-pizza-chain-tech-company/1422647).
- **Step 2, 4.2–4.5 stars.** Spiegel: purchase likelihood peaks between 4.2 and 4.5 stars and falls toward a perfect 5:
  [Practical Ecommerce](https://www.practicalecommerce.com/Study-5-Star-Reviews-Not-Necessarily-Helpful) · PowerReviews above.
  Some summaries say 4.0–4.7; we use the more common 4.2–4.5.
- **Step 3.** The 62% is PwC's above; when and how to ask for a review is our advice.
- **Step 4, Buffett's memo.** Sent to Berkshire's managers every two years for more than 25 years: "We can afford to lose money,
  even a lot of money. But we can't afford to lose reputation, even a shred of reputation":
  [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/08/06/one-page-memo-billionaire-warren-buffett-sent-to-berkshire-hathaway-managers-every-two-years-for-over-25-years-reputation-over-profit) ·
  [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/general/2015/01/09/the-3-things-warren-buffett-requested-from-his-all.aspx).
  Then Mrs. B's motto (part 1's sources).

## 6 · The traps
- **Trap 1, Tylenol (1982).** Seven people died in the Chicago area after taking cyanide-laced Tylenol capsules; the bottles had
  been tampered with on store shelves: [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/health/tylenol-murders-1982). Johnson & Johnson
  recalled 31 million bottles at a cost of about $100 million:
  [Retail Brew](https://www.retailbrew.com/stories/2022/10/13/total-recall-how-the-tylenol-murders-of-1982-ushered-in-tamper-resistant-packaging).
  Disputed share figures: 37% → 7% → 30% within a year ([CEI](https://cei.org/?p=52558)); 35% → under 8% (PBS); back to 30%,
  and to 35% by the end of 1983 ([journalism.university](https://journalism.university/?p=245078)). Safer wording:
  «أكثر من الثلث» → «أقل من 10%» → «خلال سنة… حوالي 30%». «من كل 3 مسكّنات… وحدة» is a third of the pain-reliever market.
  Why it came back: J&J hid nothing and returned with tamper-resistant packaging, seals that show if a pack was opened
  (Retail Brew above: the murders "ushered in tamper-resistant packaging") → «علب جديدة… فيها أقفال تبيّن لك إذا أحد
  فتحها قبلك».
  Saudi Arabia: 32.2 million people in the 2022 census ([Asharq Al-Awsat](https://english.aawsat.com/gulf/4358341-saudi-population-32-million-51-under-30) ·
  [AGBI](https://www.agbi.com/economy/2023/06/average-age-in-saudi-is-29-as-2022-population-hits-32-2m/)); 31 ÷ 32.2 = 96% →
  «تقريبًا علبة… لكل واحد في السعودية». Khalid's car wash in Jeddah is an illustration, labelled [مثال توضيحي].
- **Trap 2, Wells Fargo.** 31 August 2017: the bank's expanded review found 3.5 million potentially unauthorized accounts
  (2009–2016): [Wells Fargo 8-K (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000072971/000007297117000428/wfcexhibit99108312017.htm) ·
  [Fox / AP](https://www.fox5atlanta.com/news/wells-fargo-says-3-5-million-accounts-involved-in-scandal). Hence «البنك نفسه
  قال… يمكن انفتحت بدون إذن».
  The scandal broke in September 2016: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wells_Fargo_cross-selling_scandal).
  February 2020: $3 billion to settle the Justice Department's and the SEC's cases; the agreed statement of facts says more than
  5,300 employees were fired between 2011 and 2015 and blames "onerous sales goals" and management pressure:
  [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/department-of-justice-wells-fargo-fake-accounts-settlement) ·
  [Washington Times / AP](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2020/feb/21/wells-fargo-pay-3b-resolve-fake-account-scandal/).
  3 × 3.75 = 11.25 → «أكثر من 11 مليار ريال». Sultan's used phones in Dammam are an illustration, labelled [مثال توضيحي].

## 10 · وقفة
- **The verse.** Al-Isra 17:34, «وَأَوْفُوا بِالْعَهْدِ ۖ إِنَّ الْعَهْدَ كَانَ مَسْئُولًا»: [Quran.com](https://quran.com/17/34).
  Our explanation follows al-Tabari: keep the contracts you make with people, «والبيوع… والإجارات، وغير ذلك من العقود», and
  «إن الله جلّ ثناؤه سائلٌ ناقضَ العهد عن نقضه إياه»:
  [Tafsir al-Tabari, 17:34](https://quran.ksu.edu.sa/tafseer/tabary/sura17-aya34.html) (check).
- **The hadith.** Sahih al-Bukhari 2079: «الْبَيِّعَانِ بِالْخِيَارِ مَا لَمْ يَتَفَرَّقَا… فَإِنْ صَدَقَا وَبَيَّنَا بُورِكَ لَهُمَا فِي
  بَيْعِهِمَا، وَإِنْ كَتَمَا وَكَذَبَا مُحِقَتْ بَرَكَةُ بَيْعِهِمَا»: [Sunnah.com](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2079). We quote its second
  half, which stands on its own, and say «رواه البخاري». «صدقا» (in the description and the price) and «بيّنا» (showed any
  flaw) follow Ibn Hajar's explanation in *Fath al-Bari* on this hadith.
- «من أكثر من 1400 سنة»: the hadith is from the Prophet's lifetime (died 632) → 2026 − 632 = 1,394 years by the solar calendar
  and more than 1,400 by the hijri one. We say «من أكثر من 1400 سنة» as people here count it.
