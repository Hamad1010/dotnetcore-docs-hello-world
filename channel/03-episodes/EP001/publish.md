## 1. Start here: the package I would publish

- **When:** your plan says launch day is **Monday 26 Oct, 8–9 pm Saudi time** (`08-coaching-roadmap.md`, `03-viral-playbook.md` §11).
- **Title 1 + thumbnail 1** (the story twist). It is the clearest promise and the one the first 40 seconds deliver.
- If your Studio shows **Test & compare**, test thumbnails **1, 3 and 5** (story · pain · how-to: three different reasons to click),
  then titles **1, 4 and 7**. Let each test run to the end (up to about 2 weeks); change nothing else meanwhile.
- Description **1** to start; the other four are for tests, the Community tab and the next uploads.
- Why this order: the niche is full of «بدون رأس مال» lists and income promises (see the audience-words file). A true story with
  numbers is what they don't have; the pain-word titles (4–6) are the second angle, the how-to titles (7–9) the third.

## 5. Should we add the accounts of the companies and people?

**Yes, in a small block called «الحسابات الرسمية (للاطلاع فقط)», and no as a growth trick.** It is already in every description above.

- **Why yes:** it shows where the facts come from, it is honest (the video really is about WhatsApp, Instagram, Kodak, Microsoft, Naval

  the video truly discusses is the opposite of that.
- **What it will not do:** YouTube gives no extra reach for linking big accounts. The gain is trust, and sometimes a featured account
  notices a respectful credit. Don't expect views from it.
- **Keep it clean:** links to official sites and public profiles only; a line saying there is no relation between us and them (it is in
  the notes block); no logos or photos of them in thumbnails (we use none); no brand or person names in the tags or title that the video
  doesn't discuss; no @-tagging many accounts in comments or posts (that is spam).
