## Hook: Brian Acton, Facebook and WhatsApp (2009 → 2014)
- Acton's tweet after Facebook turned down his job application, 3 August 2009 ("Facebook turned me down. It was a
  great opportunity to connect with some fantastic people. Looking forward to life's next adventure.") and a Twitter
  rejection on 23 May 2009:
  [BGR](https://bgr.com/2014/02/20/whatsapp-messaging-app-history/) ·
  [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/technology%2Fmedia%2Fgot-rejected-for-a-job-dont-lose-heart-1.1293880)
- Facebook's deal to buy WhatsApp, announced 19 February 2014: $19 billion headline price
  ($4B cash + $12B stock + $3B restricted stock for founders and staff), 55 employees, more than 450 million monthly users:
  [NPR](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2014/02/19/279763675/facebook-will-buy-whatsapp-message-service-for-19-billion) ·
  [InformationWeek](https://www.informationweek.com/it-leadership/facebook-s-whatsapp-buy-10-staggering-stats) ·
  [Fox Business](https://foxbusiness.com/features/facebook-to-snatch-up-whatsapp-in-19b-deal)
- Disputed/careful wording: the cash-and-stock split differs between outlets (some say $16B + $3B). We say only
  "19 مليار دولار". Aug 2009 → Feb 2014 is about 4.5 years, so we say "أقل من خمس سنين". WhatsApp was co-founded by
  Jan Koum and Brian Acton, so we say "الشركة اللي أسسها مع صاحبه". Facebook rejected him once (plus Twitter), and
  paid the money *for the company*: the script says "دفعت… عشان تشتري الشركة", not "دفعت له".

## Touchable numbers (arithmetic)
- $19 billion × 3.75 (riyal peg) = 71.25 billion riyals. One riyal per second: 71.25 billion ÷ 31,557,600 seconds
  a year ≈ 2,258 years → "أكثر من 2000 سنة".
- Alternatives in the draft note: $19B ÷ 450M users ≈ $42 per user (InformationWeek gives the same); $19B ÷ 55 ≈
  $345 million per employee.
- 450 million ÷ 55 ≈ 8.2 million users per employee. Saudi population (2022 census, GASTAT): 32,175,224
  ([Saudipedia](https://saudipedia.com/تعداد-السعودية-2022) · [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/ampArticle/2134797)).
  8.2 ÷ 32.2 ≈ 25% → "تقريبًا ربع سكان السعودية".

## The problem: long working hours (WHO/ILO, 17 May 2021, data for 2016)
- 745,000 deaths from stroke and ischaemic heart disease in 2016 linked to working 55+ hours a week (398,000 stroke,
  347,000 heart disease); 479–488 million people worked 55+ hours a week:
  [ILO](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/long-working-hours-can-increase-deaths-heart-disease-and-stroke-say-ilo-and) ·
  [ScienceAlert](https://www.sciencealert.com/working-long-hours-is-killing-hundreds-of-thousands-of-people-who-study-finds)
- Careful wording: the releases also say "9% of the global population", which doesn't match the counts, so we say
  "قرابة نص مليار إنسان". 480 million ÷ 32.2 million ≈ 15 → "تقريبًا 15 ضعف سكان السعودية".
  745,000 ÷ 366 days (2016) ≈ 2,035 a day → "أكثر من 2000 إنسان كل يوم".

## Comparison: Kodak and Instagram (2012)
- Kodak employed 140,000–145,000 people at its peak (sources differ on the year) and filed for bankruptcy in January
  2012; about three months later (9 April 2012) Facebook bought Instagram, 13 employees, for $1 billion:
  [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/technology%2Fdigital-killed-the-photography-star-1.1006897) ·
  [DIYPhotography](https://www.diyphotography.net/kodak-instagram-tale-dollars-sense/)
- We say "أكثر من 140 ألف". 13 = a football team (11) + 2 substitutes.
