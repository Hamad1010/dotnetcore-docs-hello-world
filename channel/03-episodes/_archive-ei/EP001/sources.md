# EP001 · Sources and fact-check

Rule: every number on screen or in the narration is backed here. "Disputed" notes say how we worded it.

## Hook: first impressions in 0.1 s
- Willis, J. & Todorov, A. (2006). *First impressions: making up your mind after a 100-ms exposure to a face.* Psychological Science. Summary: [Princeton news, 22 Aug 2006](https://princeton.edu/news/2006/08/22/snap-judgments-decide-faces-character-psychologist-finds) · [Princeton research portal](https://collaborate.princeton.edu/en/publications/first-impressions-making-up-your-mind-after-a-100-ms-exposure-to-/)
- Note: the study measured *agreement* of snap judgments with unhurried judgments, not their accuracy. We say "people form their impression", not "people are right".

## The lesson: warmth is judged before competence
- Fiske, S. T., Cuddy, A. J. C. & Glick, P. (2007). *Universal dimensions of social cognition: warmth and competence.* Trends in Cognitive Sciences 11(2), 77–83. Warmth (intent) is judged first and weighs more, then competence (ability).

## Story 1: Princess Diana, London, 19 April 1987
- Opened the UK's first purpose-built HIV/AIDS unit at Middlesex Hospital and shook a patient's hand without gloves in front of the press: [PinkNews](https://thepinknews.com/2017/08/31/20-years-after-her-death-how-princess-diana-transformed-global-attitudes-to-hiv-aids) · [Logically fact-check](https://www.logically.ai/factchecks/all/bcc83422) · [LGBT History UK](https://lgbthistoryuk.org/wiki/Princess_Diana)
- Disputed: patient count/name differ by source. We don't name the patient. "Millions of people" = the global press coverage. The nickname ("Queen/Princess of hearts") came later. Wording: "one of the reasons people call her…".

## Story 5: Japan at the Qatar World Cup, 23 November 2022
- Japan beat Germany 2–1; FIFA posted the spotless dressing room with origami cranes and a "thank you" note; fans cleaned the stands: [Al Arabiya](https://english.alarabiya.net/sports/2022/11/24/Japan-fans-tidy-Qatar-stadium-players-leave-thank-you-note-after-beating-Germany) · [News9/AP](https://news9.com/story/6382907e64ccb3070eda39fc/after-shock-world-cup-win-japanese-players-leave-dressing-room-spotless) · [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/qatar-world-cup-2022/news-views/fifa-world-cup-qatar-japan-wins-opening-match-against-germany-japanese-fans-leave-stadium-spotless-1.1669202177522)
- Disputed: the note's language (Japanese only vs Japanese + Arabic) and the number of cranes. We say "a note saying thank you" and "small colored paper birds".

## Story 6: Hamdi Ulukaya / Chobani, April 2016
- 2,000 full-time employees received shares worth up to 10% of the company, payable on sale/IPO; average reported ~$150,000; around 30% of the workforce were refugees/immigrants: [CNN Money](https://money.cnn.com/2016/04/26/pf/chobani/) · [NPR](https://www.npr.org/sections/thesalt/2016/04/28/476021520/why-chobani-gave-employees-a-financial-stake-in-companys-future) · [ILCTR profile](https://www.ilctr.org/about-immigrants/immigrant-entrepreneurs/hall-of-fame/hamdi-ulukaya/)

## Story 8: Ritz-Carlton and "Joshie the giraffe", 2012
