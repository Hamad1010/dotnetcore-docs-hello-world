# Launch emails for the five guides

> Written 11 Nov 2026 with `/yt-seo` (subject lines in the words people use) and a `humanizer` pass. Who gets what:
> days 0, 3 and 7 go from the email tool (MailerLite or Brevo) **only** to people who ticked the consent box or signed up
> themselves; day 10 goes to the guide's buyers through a Gumroad Workflow (it is about their purchase). Gumroad sends
> Workflow emails only after the account has earned $100 after fees and had one payout
> ([`17-gumroad-store.md`](../../01-plan/17-gumroad-store.md) §7).
>
> Day 3 shares one scenario from the guide, free. Its text is not here (the repository is public): it is in
> `07-products/out/store/day3-samples.md`, which `build_store.py` fills from the guide, and in the zip you received.
> Sender name: «غرفة الفرص». [الرابط] = the product link; [الرابط]/LAUNCH = the same link with the launch code.

| Day | When | Subject length |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | the video's day, an hour after it goes live | under 40 characters, so the phone shows it all |
| 3 | three days later, same hour | " |
| 7 | the last evening of the launch price | " |
| 10 | Gumroad Workflow: 10 days after each purchase | " |
